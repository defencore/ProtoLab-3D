# Library structure

Browse by function, then component family, then choose a model or size in the configurator. Product variants remain inside their component; IDs are stable.

The sidebar and preset browser share this hierarchy. Search includes categories, part names, model names and source designations. Generic prototypes are separate from sourced catalog dimensions; see the [engineering coverage audit](engineering-library-coverage.md) for limitations.

## STRUCTURAL PARTS

- **ALUMINIUM PROFILES**: Modular T-slot extrusion; T-slot aluminium profile.
- **STEEL & GENERAL SECTIONS**: Structural section.
- **BRACKETS & MOUNTS**: Angle bracket; Bent sheet bracket.
- **BOXES & CASES**: Open enclosure.
- **HANDLES HINGES & FEET**: Machine frame hardware.
- **CABLE MANAGEMENT**: Cable duct and drag chain.
- **WHEELS & ROLLERS**: Robot and pneumatic wheel; Tracked running gear; Utility wheel.
