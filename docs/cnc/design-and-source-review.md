# ProtoLab CNC: independent design and source review

The library machine is an independently dimensioned bolted **ProtoLab CNC** with 800 x 1500 x 200 mm travel. It targets D16T/duralumin and other nonferrous prototypes with a complete **0.05 mm finished-part tolerance field, or ±0.025 mm**. This is an unverified requirement, not a claimed capability of the preview or exported CAD. See [mechanical qualification](mechanical-qualification.md) and [precision feed drives](precision-drive-selection.md) for the load assumptions and remaining acceptance work.

The [CNC Machines UA product and configurator](https://cncmachines.com.ua/uk/product/yastreb-8015-200/) were inspected on 15 October 2026. The source is a research reference, not the identity of the library machine. Undisclosed factory fabrication drawings were not supplied. Factory envelope, mass and advertised positioning accuracy are not assigned to this much heavier DIY arrangement. Improved source-selected equipment and modeled interfaces do not establish that the complete machine is better than the reference; rigidity, thermal behavior, loaded accuracy and manufacturing effort need measured comparison.

## Compact head and real load paths

The portal beam center is 400 mm above the table. The fixed Z tower ends 220 mm above it and extends to 770 mm; its 380 mm web width provides real rail, bridge and encoder mounting stock. It is machined as one U blank with a 20 mm web, 12 mm fins of 80 mm depth and R5 roots. The narrower tower and central drive reflect the arrangement visible in the user's front/side photographs without inventing hidden OEM dimensions.

The moving saddle is a separate 226 mm-wide one-piece U blank with a 30 mm web and 80 mm fins. Its height follows the actual spindle clamp datum and selected tool gauge. End stock now ends at tableTop+545 mm and begins at clampLow+62 mm; guide/clamp interfaces,200 mm stroke and220 mm fixed-head clearance are retained. Native slices disclose the small local net-section change from relocating the maximum-limit M4 holes. A 90 x 63 x 30 mm central nut boss replaces the old eccentric arm. Source FDI nut, shaft, flange and BK15/BF15 envelopes receive explicit machined pockets, including an 80 x 24 mm rear channel leaving 6 mm front skin. A 220 mm trial intersected eight carriage cap heads at its R5 roots; 226 mm retains those roots and clears the actual heads. The central screw is X=0, Y=178 mm. Real 5 mm steel shims mount the support feet directly to the fixed web; a removable top bridge supports three servo columns. No detached side boss, fictional support pocket or extra lower platform is used to hide an intersection.

The [boxed portal](portal-assembly.md) replaces the two full-height solid cheeks with short100 x300 x8 mm steel posts,25 mm aluminium flanges and separate integral T-shaped lower drive links. The beam bears on horizontal flange faces; link screws remain accessible from below. The original X motor datum uses a short left end plate. The right beam-end insert is removed. The X scale uses a separate backing plate attached to the flat rear wall, retaining a complete spar bearing face above the rounded corner.

The fixed table is1800 mm long and centered atY+270 mm. A shallow drip tray ends25 mm below the table ribs; no rib passes through its walls. All twelve hangers have real floor/rib bearing seats. It collects drips and requires drainage/leak qualification and a separate splash enclosure for flood coolant. Six1100 mm rounded100 x100 x8 mm steel ribs carry ten40 mm aluminium slats. The steel beam remains1375 x200 x200 x12.5 mm RHS with a machined front rail datum. Two300 x100 x10 mm steel bed members and separate30 mm lower flanges carry the moving weight. This is an independent design, not the source machine's mass or fabrication drawing.

## Travel, stock and tool setup

Tool projection from the spindle nose is 20-50 mm, default 25 mm. The reference tip spans tableTop..tableTop+200 over the actual 200 mm stroke. The fixed head starts at least 220 mm above the table. A 200 mm stock slab has 20 mm fixed-head clearance but no upward cutter retract at its top. With 20 mm retract, allow at most 180 mm stock before fixture, holder and workholding allowances. A configured gauge is a clearance assumption; it does not qualify cutter stiffness or collet runout.

Assembly, axis minimum/maximum and Z assembly / details are finite inspection states. Canonical manufacturing/procurement identities remain unchanged when parts are posed or separated. The model does not implement continuous internally phased joint motion or a toolpath simulator.

`scripts/audit-cnc-workspace.mts` checks actual native installed solids against an 800 x 1500 mm stock slab at heights 5/50/100/200 mm in nine XY positions. `scripts/audit-cnc-milling.mts` checks the installed assembly and travel corners. Reports retain exact native source, runtime, configuration, occurrence and CAD checksum guards. They establish only cases recorded as passed for the current source; earlier geometry reports remain historical evidence. Individual compact-head proofs do not replace the final complete-machine check.

See `test-results/cnc-milling/assembly-native.json`, `Simple.FCStd` and `Full.FCStd` for the complete-machine execution records, and `workspace-native.json` for stock checks. A passed status supports this revision only when its source/runtime/configuration/CAD guards match. The former1364-leaf,3390 Simple/3794 Full checks and216 stock poses describe the preceding solid-cheek snapshot and are historical after the portal/control/tray changes. Positive intended-face contact and attachment proofs are recorded separately under `mounting/`; absence of an overlap alone does not establish mounting, rigidity or sealed containment.

For expensive long screws, native intersections can localize the actual installed solid with a box enclosing its entire partner before taking their Common. This removes only geometry that cannot intersect the partner and preserves the original 0.001 mm³ criterion, finite-volume and topology checks. It does not substitute a smooth shaft or a thread coupon. The recorded operator/version and exact CAD guards are required for reused cases; contradictory whole-body kernel results are preserved separately.

## Separate manufacture and purchasing

Every fabricated frame blank, sleeve, washer and bolt is a configured library occurrence. Purchased screw/nut kits retain one matched-set purchasing identity with two actual installed leaves. Supports, motors, couplings, rails/blocks, spindle, feedback, controls and cooling retain exact source executions or explicit unknown-order limitations. Supplier internal drawings are not reverse-engineered into a home-build BOM.

Open each physical detail in the assembly tree to prepare its own drawing, material certificate, stock allowance, datum scheme, fits, thread class, finish, torque/preload and inspection plan. Simple and Full macros are editable CAD models; they are not released drawings or CAM instructions. Full thread fit uses an explicit 0.05 mm radial inspection allowance on authored CNC female threads while preserving nominal callouts. It is not an ISO tolerance class or a new procurement size.

The current stock routes require long-bed machining or a released segmented-alignment process. The 2400 mm bed datums cannot all be finish-machined in one setup on this 1500 mm-travel machine. Integral U blanks need verified billet availability, residual-stress control, milling access and CMM/datum inspection. Bolted bed flanges and end inserts need qualified no-slip load transfer. Guards, chip covers, foundation/leveling, lubrication, actual cooler flow/temperature, electrical protection, limits, emergency stop and power-loss Z retention still need a complete commissioning design.

### Priorities for the current design

1. Define real fixtures, stock heights and cutter gauges before changing head clearance or Z stiffness. Any lower-clearance option must retain its own valid travel envelope.
2. Release the master/secondary rail datum sequence, indicator locations, support alignment and tightening inspection. Add assembly aids as separate manufactured library details after their actual interfaces are designed.
3. Review removable chip covers, cable/hose bend envelopes, lubrication access and replacement access. Include their friction and lost travel in verification.
4. Design repeatable fixture datums and a qualified toolsetting arrangement. Include their height, clamping deformation and measurement uncertainty in the existing error budget.
5. Record force-displacement tests at multiple XY/Z positions, bidirectional positioning, spindle tram/runout, warmed-up drift and circle/diagonal coupon results. Finishing parameters must be established for the actual D16T alloy, tool, fixture and spindle.

These are design-review decisions rather than transferred CAD. Preserve a single source revision when preparing a build: the examined Milo repository revision `cecc5a616a1b326f50f9dd881eaa79c9e7506039` contains a STEP export dated 7 May 2023, while current documentation revision `6f510d0d620c0a8f83ec067a7def668e779b80fb` describes separate Milo 1.5/2.0 variants. IndyMill's repository and current website BOM differ. Inspect the applicable asset licence before importing any source models or distributing adaptations; general build ideas do not resolve a model's provenance or technical qualification.
