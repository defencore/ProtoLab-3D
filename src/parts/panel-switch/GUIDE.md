# Toggle switches & buttons

Find **Electronics → Switches & buttons → Toggle switches & buttons**. The catalog is listed directly in this category.

Select an identified purchased switch. Dimensions are fixed; filters select a different article rather than stretching a component. The catalogue contains C&K 7000 toggles, E-Switch R1966 rockers, C&K A rotary selectors, E-Switch PVA6/PVA6L panel buttons, Omron B3F tactile buttons, an APEM 12146AK toggle with an optional 90C46 guard, and the DAIER ASW-07D illuminated toggle either alone or paired with a transparent SAC-01 flip cover.

Z=0 is the mounting datum for bushing switches and the PCB top for tactile buttons. For ASW-07D it is the case face below the bushing; the panel's rear face is Z=3 mm above the lower retaining nut. The visible body, mounting bushing or snap-in bezel, actuator and external terminal portions remain separate inspection components. Threads are nominal cylindrical interfaces. Internal contacts and terminal portions embedded in the insulating case are omitted.

First, center and last describe mechanical positions. Two-position switches reject center. Parentheses in a function such as OFF–(ON) identify momentary action: the pressed position requires external force. A rotary selector turns by the source's 30° index. Its selected solder-terminal population follows its circuit; no knob is supplied.

## Mounting

- C&K 7000 S/Y/Z variants retain the 1/4-40 nominal bushing, 8.89 mm bushing height, 10.67 mm lever and SPDT/DPDT case and terminal differences. Use the manufacturer's full panel and anti-rotation pattern, including tolerance.
- R1966 uses a rectangular snap-in opening. The recorded 19.2 × 13 mm minimum belongs to a thin panel; the opening length must increase with panel thickness according to the source table. The bezel is 20.8 × 14.8 mm. A similar-looking KCD1 does not establish interchangeability.
- C&K A uses a Ø9.80 mm panel opening for the 3/8-32 bushing plus the manufacturer's Ø3.18 mm anti-rotation feature. The D-flat shaft remains visible. The model omits the anti-rotation pin, so obtain its location from the drawing.
- PVA6/PVA6L uses a Ø16.0–16.2 mm opening with a 15.4–15.6 mm orientation flat. The latching PVA6L body has source-dimensioned 15.3 mm flats. The momentary PVA6 body flats are not dimensioned: its modeled 15.3 mm is an illustrative clearance choice within the source panel opening. Confirm that detail on the purchased article. Permissible panel thickness differs by bezel execution; consult the selected drawing. The separate four solder terminals represent two switch and two LED connections. Colour indicates the ordered LED option, without an electrical simulation.
- B3F is a PCB switch. The 6 × 6 mm models have a 6.5 × 4.5 mm board-hole centre pattern. B3F-4000 has a 12.5 × 5 mm pattern. Four pins provide two common pairs, not four independent circuits.
- DAIER ASW-07D follows the manufacturer's [ASW-07D-1 drawing](https://www.chinadaier.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/ASW-07D-1.pdf): 28 × 16 × 19 mm case, M12 × 0.75 bushing with 12.5 mm projection, Ø7 mm actuator with 19.9 mm projection and 24° total travel. Its Ø12.2 mm panel opening has an orientation tongue; the nominal circular hole alone is incomplete. Two lower 6.3 × 0.8 mm switch blades have 24.2 mm centres; a third horizontal blade is the lamp ground. The public product page contains a different overall envelope and conflicting current values, so the linked drawing execution controls geometry and no current rating is assigned. ASW-07D-2 remains a different model.

For the bare ASW-07D, **Panel thickness** adjusts the installation from 0.3 to 4 mm. The lower nut stays at Z=0.6–3.0 mm, the panel occupies Z=3.0 to Z=3.0 plus its thickness, and the upper locking nut starts at the panel's front face. A 3 mm panel therefore occupies Z=3–6 mm, with the upper nut at Z=6–8.4 mm. The default 0.3 mm retains the original nut placement. This control moves the upper nut only: the case, bushing, actuator, terminals and purchased model identity stay fixed. The guarded variant retains its separate fixed guard-adjustment stack.

## Protective cover

DAIER ASW-07D + SAC-01 is the rounded transparent flip-cover assembly. Select red, green, yellow or blue to match the manufacturer's paired SKUs **ASW-07D+SAC-01-TR**, **-TG**, **-TY** and **-TBL**. The colour changes the LED lens and transparent cap; dimensions stay fixed. The first position is OFF. Opening the cap permits either toggle position; closing returns it to OFF.

The 28 mm case length, actuator travel and guard length follow X. The guard's free front and OFF position face −X; its hinge sits at +X with the hinge pin along Y. The base's orientation tongue faces the free front and fits the bushing's −X groove, following the manufacturer's mounting views.

The [SAC-01 drawing](https://www.chinadaier.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/SAC-01.pdf) dimensions its 46.6 mm closed length, 27.8 mm height above the base seat, 40.6 mm base length, 17 / 15 mm base widths, 0.9 mm sheet and Ø12 mm keyed hole. Its hinge axis is 11.8 mm from the base's rear end. The model retains these dimensions and approximates the drawing's 45 mm open envelope within 0.05 mm. The rounded cover silhouette is reconstructed from the drawing; shell thickness, hole-to-hinge distance, hinge pin and 69° inspection angle are illustrative. The spring is omitted. The cover seat is adjusted 9.5 mm above the switch case with two nuts and a washer to clear the selected actuator; that installation height is illustrative rather than a dimensioned panel stack. Check it on the purchased assembly. Coloured transparency uses illustrative 55% opacity in the preview; native export keeps the same surfaces and RGB colours.

APEM 90C46 is a red, keyway-left guard that holds the toggle down when closed. It is a separately ordered accessory paired with the Ø11.9/12 mm bushing class of the 12146AK. It is not attached to the miniature 6.35 mm C&K toggles. Two retaining nuts must set the guard height on a physical installation.

The APEM body envelope, guard shell, hinge, open angle, keyway and adjustment details are photo-derived approximations, explicitly classified as envelope geometry. They permit an initial packaging study but do not establish purchased-article fit or a verified safety interlock. Closed holds the first position; selecting another position opens the cover. The shell has an open bottom, side walls and a fixed hinge, so opening moves the cover as one rigid component.

## Evidence and scope

Each catalogue record links a manufacturer-authored drawing or product page, identifies the verified model identity and lists its geometric limits. This package reconstructs source-dimensioned interfaces; it does not redistribute original manufacturer CAD. Public CAD buttons were investigated, but available manufacturer endpoints required a sign-in or exposed no public STEP link. No community model is presented as manufacturer CAD.

Case radii, rocker concavity, snap flexures, contact mechanisms, terminal holes, nut profiles and thread helices are simplified. No current, voltage, ingress or lifetime rating is guessed from shape. Check the exact purchased revision and complete order designation before cutting a panel or selecting a circuit.
