# Toggle switches & buttons

Find **Electronics → Switches & buttons → Toggle switches & buttons**. The catalog is listed directly in this category.

Select an identified purchased switch. Dimensions are fixed; filters select a different article rather than stretching a component. The catalogue contains C&K 7000 toggles, E-Switch R1966 rockers, C&K A rotary selectors, E-Switch PVA6/PVA6L and DAIER DS-225 panel buttons, four provisional BERM BEM-LA16 buttons, Omron B3F tactile buttons, an APEM 12146AK toggle with an optional 90C46 guard, and the DAIER ASW-07D illuminated toggle either alone or paired with a transparent SAC-01 flip cover.

Z=0 is the mounting datum for bushing switches and the PCB top for tactile buttons. For ASW-07D it is the case face below the bushing; the panel's rear face is Z=3 mm above the lower retaining nut. The visible body, mounting bushing or snap-in bezel, actuator and external terminal portions remain separate inspection components. Threads are nominal cylindrical interfaces. Internal contacts and terminal portions embedded in the insulating case are omitted.

First, center and last describe mechanical positions. Two-position switches reject center. Parentheses in a function such as OFF–(ON) identify momentary action: the pressed position requires external force. A rotary selector turns by the source's 30° index. Its selected solder-terminal population follows its circuit; no knob is supplied.

**Mounting hardware** defaults to both nuts and the lower washer. ## Protective cover

DAIER ASW-07D + SAC-01 is the rounded transparent flip-cover assembly. Select red, green, yellow or blue to match the manufacturer's paired SKUs **ASW-07D+SAC-01-TR**, **-TG**, **-TY** and **-TBL**. The colour changes the LED lens and transparent cap; dimensions stay fixed. The first position is OFF. Opening the cap permits either toggle position; closing returns it to OFF.

The 28 mm case length, actuator travel and guard length follow X. The guard's free front and OFF position face −X; its hinge sits at +X with the hinge pin along Y. The base's orientation tongue faces the free front and fits the bushing's −X groove, following the manufacturer's mounting views.

The [SAC-01 drawing](https://www.chinadaier.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/SAC-01.pdf) dimensions its 46.6 mm closed length, 27.8 mm height above the base seat, 40.6 mm base length, 17 / 15 mm base widths, 0.9 mm sheet and Ø12 mm keyed hole. Its hinge axis is 11.8 mm from the base's rear end. The model retains these dimensions and approximates the drawing's 45 mm open envelope within 0.05 mm. The rounded cover silhouette is reconstructed from the drawing; shell thickness, hole-to-hinge distance, hinge pin and 69° inspection angle are illustrative. The spring is omitted. The cover seat is adjusted 9.5 mm above the switch case with two nuts and a washer to clear the selected actuator; that installation height is illustrative rather than a dimensioned panel stack. Check it on the purchased assembly. Coloured transparency uses illustrative 55% opacity in the preview; native export keeps the same surfaces and RGB colours.

APEM 90C46 is a red, keyway-left guard that holds the toggle down when closed. It is a separately ordered accessory paired with the Ø11.9/12 mm bushing class of the 12146AK. It is not attached to the miniature 6.35 mm C&K toggles. Two retaining nuts must set the guard height on a physical installation.

The APEM body envelope, guard shell, hinge, open angle, keyway and adjustment details are photo-derived approximations, explicitly classified as envelope geometry. They permit an initial packaging study but do not establish purchased-article fit or a verified safety interlock. Closed holds the first position; selecting another position opens the cover. The shell has an open bottom, side walls and a fixed hinge, so opening moves the cover as one rigid component.

## Evidence and scope

Each catalogue record links a manufacturer-authored drawing, product page, supplier listing or identified user attachment and lists its geometric limits. BERM records establish supplier-listed identities only, with assumed envelope geometry. Other records reconstruct source-dimensioned interfaces or explicitly approximate envelopes; this package does not redistribute original manufacturer CAD. Public CAD buttons were investigated, but available manufacturer endpoints required a sign-in or exposed no public STEP link. No community model is presented as manufacturer CAD.

Case radii, rocker concavity, snap flexures, contact mechanisms, terminal holes, nut profiles and thread helices are simplified. No current, voltage, ingress or lifetime rating is guessed from shape. Check the exact purchased revision and complete order designation before cutting a panel or selecting a circuit.
