# Cable duct and drag chain

Open cable duct and articulated rectangular cable carrier reference.

Find **STRUCTURAL PARTS → CABLE MANAGEMENT → Cable duct and drag chain**.

## Variants

- Cable duct · 25 × 18 mm
- Drag chain · 25 × 18 mm

## Controls

Carrier form; Outside width; Outside height; Length; Wall thickness; Link count; Model detail.

## Scope

Straight carrier only; link hinges, bend stops and minimum bend radius are not defined. Dimensions are editable prototype choices, not source-certified product dimensions or a manufacturing drawing. Threads are smooth nominal envelopes unless explicitly stated. No load, pressure or service-life rating is implied.

Separate named components are exported to FreeCAD; the exploded state separates them along Z. The preview and native export share the same shape recipe. This is an independent, editable package.
