# Compact pneumatic cylinders

AirTAC ACE32 with 25 or 50 mm stroke, double acting, female M8x1.25 rod end. Published body length is 44 + stroke; retracted rod projection is 7 mm. Mounting pitch is 32.5 mm. The four corner D4.5 paths are modeled; confirm the delivered mounting arrangement before drilling a fixture. Specify magnetic piston and compatible sensors where position confirmation is required.

Buy the complete cylinder. Do not fabricate a pressure body from this model. Use a guided shoe and a spherical or floating rod connection to avoid lateral rod loading. Extension force is pressure times piston area, before seal friction and line losses.

ACE50 has B65.5, AC45.5, AB8, D16, JB46.5, corner D5.5 and a female M10x1.5 rod thread to depth20. The saw fixture uses this larger bore for its preliminary grip-force allocation.

The machine uses magnetic-piston, G-port order configurations: ACE50X25SG for clamps, ACE32X50SG for the admission gate, ACE50X200SG for the twin receiver lift, and ACE50X100SG for the scrap selector. The [AirTAC ACE catalogue](https://as-en.airtac.com/upload/TACE-202003240444019596.PDF) lists the series dimensions and stroke range. These are catalogue selections; local stock and lead time need a supplier quotation. Stroke extensions reuse the catalogue body-length formula, not a fabricated pressure-vessel design.
