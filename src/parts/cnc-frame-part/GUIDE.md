# CNC fabricated frame details

Each configuration represents one hollow-section member, machined plate, datum pad, bracket or turned compression sleeve. The machine uses separate library configurations for joined members and all fasteners. Local machining coordinates are measured from the base blank center; an integral rail-pad shoulder extends above that base in positive Z and requires a thicker billet. Cylinders extend along the positive indicated axis. Threads require an authored pitch and designation. Slots and counterbores retain explicit lengths and seating depths.

The material selector is a design choice. Specify the actual grade, alloy/temper, stock condition, manufacturing tolerances and drawing before production. Long rail seats require referenced machining and inspection. Hollow structural members use independent compression sleeves under through-bolts. No factory fabrication dimensions or welding drawings are claimed.

The `encoder-offset-angle` is one authored billet with a lower readhead datum wall, a connected bridge, an offset spine and an upper mounting foot. `rearWebHeight` sets the lower wall height and `shoulderWidth` sets the spine offset. The bridge retains 1 mm of geometric overlap with the lower wall. Machine the open pockets from the sides; specify feasible internal cutter radii and inspect the full moving clearance to rib-bolt heads.

`readheadEar` explicitly adds connected rectangular stock only to a readhead angle. Its origin and size are local machining coordinates, and the manufacturing blank includes its actual extent. The CNC adapters use a 1.8 mm stepped front ear with a 1 mm overlap into the recessed main wall. Full 6 mm M4 taps remain complete. With the configured radial allowance, upper edge stock is 0.45 mm over the rear 4.2 mm of depth and 1.65 mm over the front 1.8 mm. Supplier contours, actual clearance, cutter radii, preload and cable/vibration loads still need qualification; the added stock is not a strength certificate or a change to OEM parts.

`threadFitAllowance` is an explicit radial inspection allowance, default 0 mm. The CNC assembly selects 0.05 mm to separate ideal helical mating flanks in CAD; preview, nominal export and native CAD use the same configured bore envelope. Thread designation and drawing diameter stay nominal. This allowance does not specify an ISO tolerance class or authorize a different procurement size; release the real fit and machining process on the component drawing.

The current CNC Z tower and moving saddle use integral U blanks. `ribThickness`, `ribDepth`, `ribCenterX` and `internalFillet` describe machined stock from one billet. The moving saddle's nut boss is integral with its web. `centralDrivePocket` explicitly defines the shaft, nut and flange bores plus the open rear support channel; the configured channel leaves a six-millimetre front skin. It is not a generic mesh subtraction: dimensions and positive boss ligaments are validated. Release machining order, actual root radii, tool reach, residual stress and datum inspection before cutting these blanks.

`cornerReliefRadius` is an explicit through-X cutter at the positive-Y/positive-Z outside corner, supported only by `nut-bracket`. The X nut bracket selects R35 to clear the actual encoder envelope. Generic bores still require retained edge stock; the corner relief is a separately authored machining feature. Compact one-sided encoder L adapters retain their actual leg stock instead of requiring a fictitious second wall.

Hollow steel stock uses declared rounded outer and inner corners. The machine's front beam datum is machined from that stock, so gross catalogue area, bending inertia and torsion must not be treated as the final drilled section. Bed flanges depend on qualified no-slip bolt transfer before their parallel-axis stiffness can be credited. Thread engagement and modeled clearance prove fit geometry only; aluminium pull-out, bolt preload and joint slip require material- and load-specific calculation and inspection.
