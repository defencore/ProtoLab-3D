## Heltec V3.2 installation datum

The manufacturer specifies a 50.2 × 25.5 × 10.2 mm module envelope. Page 14 of the linked datasheet dimensions the OLED glass at 33.28 × 18.56 mm, active length 27.28 mm, and 18 header positions at 2.54 mm pitch. The active width of 13.64 mm is inferred from the 128 × 64 pixel aspect ratio. The board outline, PCB thickness, connector positions and all individual component heights remain packaging approximations.

The board is centered in XY, with the OLED facing +Z and USB-C facing +Y. The complete assembled model spans Z=0..10.2 mm; PCB bottom/top are Z=3.6/5.2 mm. OLED glass center is Y=-6.1 mm, with its active face at Z=10.2 mm. The approximate USB shell face is Y=26 mm, 0.9 mm past the nominal PCB end; its center is Z=2 mm. USB shell width/height are 8.8/3.2 mm. Provide a separate clearance for the larger mating plug.

The 36 header pads are unpopulated. No dedicated screw mounting holes are claimed; retain the board by its edges. Keep the WiFi spring antenna free from pressure and leave space for an external LoRa U.FL lead. The antenna clearance envelope is deliberately simple. Confirm the actual revision and battery dimensions before manufacturing a fitted enclosure. The [manufacturer product page](https://heltec.org/project/wifi-lora-32-v3/) and [pin photograph](https://heltec.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/pin.png) provide connector evidence.

PCB/body dimensions are supplier values; protruding connectors are included in rendered bounds separately. Undimensioned component positions, heights, corner radii and contacts are approximate. Use the linked drawing and physical hardware to confirm mounting and connector clearance. Flexible leads, external antennas, mating plugs and accessories are excluded. The model is a mechanical packaging reference, not a PCB fabrication file or electrical pinout.

Numeric filters omit unknown dimensions and ratings. Input-current, output-current and per-motor ESC ratings are distinct. Memory/firmware-only alternatives do not generate additional models. Semtech is the radio chip supplier, not the manufacturer of every LoRa board.

Preview and FreeCAD use the same independently movable colored solids. Package-private geometry helpers keep this part independently exportable. Validate with `npm run parts:check`, `npm run typecheck:tests`, `tests/electronics.test.ts` and `scripts/verify-electronics.ts` / `scripts/verify-electronics.py`.

## Heltec V4 installation datum

The separate `heltec-wifi-lora32-v4` catalog record follows the V4.3.1 datasheet, page 15. Its module envelope is 51.69 × 25.40 × 10.70 mm without header pins (15.60 mm with pins installed). The protected OLED frame is 33.00 × 18.60 mm with a 22.00 × 11.40 mm aperture. The official V4.3.1 STEP gives four PCB bores of 2.80 mm, at normalized X=±7 and Y=-20.0345/8.9655 mm. The datasheet annotates 1.90 mm in the protective-frame fastener area. Auxiliary pad positions and 0.90 mm bores also follow the STEP, arranged as two two-pin sections in one far-end row. PCB thickness, connector footprints and individual component envelopes remain approximate. Use the edge cradle until physical hardware confirms the mounting pattern.

The V4 PCB is centered in XY, with USB facing +Y and protected OLED facing +Z. The PCB bottom/top remain Z=3.6/5.2 mm; the complete model spans Z=0..10.7 mm. The outer PC guard is centered at X=0, Y=-5.35 mm and ends at Z=10.7 mm. The OLED glass, active face and aperture within that guard are centered at X=+2 mm, Y=-6.35 mm to match the user-requested handheld lid corrections: 2 mm left and a further 1 mm toward the power end in the exterior working view. These display positions are a user fit calibration, not a newly verified manufacturer datum. The PCB, USB and outer protective frame keep their existing positions. The display face is slightly recessed at Z=10.6 mm. USB-C is on the OLED side of the PCB: approximate mating face Y=26.6 mm, center Z=6.69 mm, shell width/height 8.8/2.98 mm. Keep room for the larger mating plug.

All 40 pads are unpopulated. Battery and solar use separate SH1.25-2 interfaces; GNSS uses SH1.25-8. The PC guard includes an integrated FPC antenna envelope, with two IPEX/U.FL sockets for LoRa and 2.4 GHz on the board. This V4 record does not contain the V3 spring antenna. Flexible antenna leads, battery, installed headers and external antennas are excluded. The [manufacturer product page](https://heltec.org/project/wifi-lora-32-v4/), [V4 documentation](https://wiki.heltec.org/docs/devices/open-source-hardware/esp32-series/lora-32/wifi-lora-32-v4/) and [official 3D resources](https://resource.heltec.cn/download/WiFi_LoRa_32_V4/3D/) support the revision scope. The V4-R8 and RF hardware revisions may differ electrically; confirm the purchased board before release.
