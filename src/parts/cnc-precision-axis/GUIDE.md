# Matched ground ballscrew set

One purchased factory-matched HIWIN ground shaft and FDI double nut order. Physical shaft and nut remain independent library instances for real assembly inspection; their geometry comes directly from those source-specific parts.

C3 and 3% preload are explicit factory order and measured acceptance requirements, never a delivered certificate. Preserve one matched-set serial/order identity and obtain the approved custom end drawing, lead/error/runout and preload/backlash torque records before acceptance. Do not procure the shaft and nut as two unrelated interchangeable generic parts.

Native shaft axis X; `nutFlangePosition` is the installed flange mating coordinate. `nutReversed` reverses the installation orientation. Source internal Gothic contact/returns/preload are opaque and Full shaft raceway is representative; the inspection bore cannot certify racecontact.
