# Catalog enclosures

Select one complete purchased enclosure under **Enclosures & devices → Catalog enclosures**. The catalog contains Altınkaya enclosure models and the Grandado-listed 83 × 58 × 35 mm flanged project box with the pictured white screw-fastened lid. Grandado is a retailer; a manufacturer is not assigned when the listing does not identify one.

Use the enclosure type, manufacturer, material, mounting, IP protection and cover filters together with minimum or maximum outside dimensions. Altınkaya's published A/B/C axis order maps to X/Y/Z, in millimetres; the source axes are retained even when a family uses a different physical orientation. The Grandado listing's 83/58/35 body dimensions map to X/Y/Z, with its complete outside X extent left unpublished because the mounting ears project beyond the body. Sorting by outside volume or one outside dimension helps compare models with complete source dimensions. The catalog does not infer usable board space from an assumed wall thickness. Characteristics missing from a source remain unpublished and do not match a value filter.

Families with several published sizes show their overall span with a **stock sizes** label. The source specifications list the discrete size choices. Dimension filters match those actual choices rather than treating every value between the endpoints as available. A family reference uses the smallest published size for its illustrative geometry; choose the required stock execution on the supplier page.

## Geometry

The Grandado model follows the retailer's photographs and the user's delivered sample: a rounded white body, a deep skirted white lid with four recessed clearance holes, four corner posts with brass inserts, four lid screws, two integral mounting ears and six short blind PCB bosses in a perimeter hexagon. The gallery's dimension image annotates the nominal closed body as **83 × 58 × 35 mm** and both a lid clearance hole and an ear mounting hole as **4 mm**. The gallery shows an opaque white lid even though the listing title claims a clear cover; the pictured execution is modeled.

The user measured the delivered lower body on 2026-11-01: **22 mm without its raised sealing rim, 23.8 mm including the rim, and 6 mm mounting-ear thickness**. The rim is therefore 1.8 mm high. Approximate reported outer/inner rim spans are 78.7 ×53.7 /76.8 ×51.6 mm; the user confirmed a roughly uniform 1 mm wall and attributed unequal inferred thicknesses to measurement error. The source model uses outer 78.7 ×53.7 mm and uniform 1 mm stock, giving modeled inner 76.7 ×51.7 mm. These delivered-sample values are not manufacturer dimensions or batch tolerances.

Local Z datums retain the nominal lower base at −17.5: the hard seating face is 4.5, the raised rim top 6.3 and the ear top −11.5. The `body-only` physical state is 110 ×58 ×23.8 mm. The native rim is one continuous dogleg loop around the existing four screw axes, built with true cylinders, quarter annuli and straight sections. Its R3.9 turns, precise molded corners and centring tolerances remain estimated. A **2 mm inferred upper support ledge** connects the inboard rim to the existing estimated lower cavity rather than leaving a thin unsupported attachment. This is a reconstruction assumption; the deeper cavity is not globally resized from the rim opening.

The original white lid retains the nominal closed top at 17.5 and overall 35 mm envelope. Its lower face moves to 4.7, retaining the previous estimated 0.2 mm hard-face gap. A recessed 1.8 mm-wide,2 mm-deep channel replaces the obsolete projecting tongue and accepts the raised rim. The original 1.6/1.0 mm outer/inner sealing-wall measurements are retained; 1 mm of inferred floor backing is represented above the channel. Original supplied screw geometry remains unchanged. Estimated inserts and blind bores move down 2 mm with the measured hard face, retaining their relative depth and the 4.6 mm insert length; these internal sizes are still unmeasured. Actual gasket profile, force, compression, leakage and production fit remain unqualified.

The body size **excludes the projecting ears**. Their extension and hole pitch are not dimensioned by the retailer. The model uses an estimated **110 × 58 × 35 mm** complete envelope, including two 13.5 mm ear projections. Its outside-length catalog characteristic stays unpublished so a maximum-length filter cannot mistake the 83 mm body for the whole mounting footprint. Search by the listed 83 × 58 × 35 mm body size or the supplier/model name. Boss locations, internal usable space, wall thickness, lid split and fastener/insert sizes are visual estimates; measure a sample before designing a mating PCB or mounting plate.

Other supplier models show a rectangular body envelope with an illustrative cavity and a separate cover envelope. Only their outer dimensions are taken from the source. Wall thickness, lid split, fasteners, hinges, flanges, panel frames, corner profiles, seals and internal mounting posts are not reconstructed supplier CAD. An exclusively clear-cover record is transparent in the preview. A family offering both clear and opaque covers uses an opaque reference; its final execution remains to be selected with the supplier. Open-only records omit the cover. Specialized DIN, rack, sloped, handheld and display shapes retain their published envelope and category, with their detailed installation geometry omitted.

For Grandado, **Cover lifted** moves the lid and its four screws by 26 mm while the body and brass inserts remain in place. FreeCAD and mesh exports keep its physical assembly closed; the reported export envelope remains 110 × 58 × 35 mm. Other models retain their earlier envelope and lifted-cover reference poses. Inspection does not change the purchased article or quantity. Internal reference solids do not create separate manufacturing items.

Use the product link attached to the selected model for the exact execution and technical drawing before releasing a PCB fit, cutout or mounting layout. An article-level IP rating is not certification of the illustrative model or of a modified enclosure.

## Catalog files

`lib/catalog-data.json` retains source records. `lib/catalog.ts` converts those records to the complete preset structure, and `presets.json` is the portable snapshot of that structure. Update and verify both together. Optional source characteristics are omitted rather than guessed. The source inventory and extraction scope are recorded in [enclosure sources](../../../docs/enclosure-sources.md).
