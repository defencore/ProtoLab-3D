# Conveyor rollers

Interroll Series 1700, steel tube D50x1.5, stationary D12 shaft with female-thread ends. Specify installation length EL and order reference RL = EL - 10. The usable steel tube is U = RL - 26. Allow the supplier end play; do not clamp the rotating bearing housings between the frame cheeks.

The complete roller is one purchased item. Its four modeled components are visual subdivisions, not four separate order lines. Verify the M8x15 shaft-end option against the supplier confirmation. Bearing and seal interiors are simplified.

The EC5000 selection represents a crowned D50 RollerDrive for belt handling, configured as a 24 V / 35 W unit. Order the matching AI controller, supply and cable interface. The model reserves installation space; the crown, shaft flats, cable exit and length relation must be checked against the ordered supplier drawing. Do not use Series 1700 shaft machining dimensions to fabricate mounts for EC5000.
