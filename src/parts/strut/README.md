# Gas spring and damper

Telescopic strut with adjustable extension and separate rod/end eyes.

Find **BEARINGS & SEALS → DAMPERS & ISOLATORS → Gas spring and damper**.

## Variants

- Telescopic strut · 150 mm body

## Controls

Body diameter; Rod diameter; Body length; Stroke; Extension; Eye bore.

## Scope

Mounting eyes are dimensional references with no swivel constraint or force model. Dimensions are editable prototype choices, not source-certified product dimensions or a manufacturing drawing. Threads are smooth nominal envelopes unless explicitly stated. No load, pressure or service-life rating is implied.

Separate named components are exported to FreeCAD; the exploded state separates them along Z. The preview and native export share the same shape recipe. This is an independent, editable package.
