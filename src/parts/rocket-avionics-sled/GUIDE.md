# Rocket avionics mounting sled

A single removable plate for planning an electronics bay for hobby rocket events such as Large Dangerous Rocket Ships (LDRS). Four through holes form a rectangular mounting pattern. No electronics, battery, rods, fasteners or bay tube are included.

## Dimensions and origin

Width is along X, length along Y and thickness along Z. The origin is at the center of the lower face. Hole centers are at `(+/- pitchX/2, +/- pitchY/2)`. Validation retains at least 2 mm of material around each hole and between adjacent holes. This is a geometric allowance, not a strength criterion.

The compact, standard and wide examples are authored prototype dimensions without supplier provenance. Their patterns do not claim compatibility with any flight controller or tracker. Enter the measured mounting pattern and check the plate's transverse diagonal, equipment height, connector access and fastener projection against the actual bay.

Preview and FreeCAD use the same shared solid feature definition. The plate has four full circular through holes. Geometry is one continuous physical detail and uses millimetres.
