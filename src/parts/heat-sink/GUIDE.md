# Finned heat sink

Extruded heat sink with editable base, fin pitch and clearance mounting holes.

Find **ELECTRONICS & VISION → COOLING & MOUNTING → Finned heat sink**.

## Variants

- Heat sink · 40 × 50 × 20 mm
- Heat sink · 80 × 100 × 30 mm

## Controls

Base width; Extrusion length; Total height; Base thickness; Number of fins; Fin thickness; Model detail.

## Scope

No heat-transfer or thermal-resistance calculation. Envelope mode intentionally fills the fin region. Dimensions are editable prototype choices, not source-certified product dimensions or a manufacturing drawing. Threads are smooth nominal envelopes unless explicitly stated. No load, pressure or service-life rating is implied.

Separate named components are exported to FreeCAD; the exploded state separates them along Z. The preview and native export share the same shape recipe. This is an independent, editable package.
