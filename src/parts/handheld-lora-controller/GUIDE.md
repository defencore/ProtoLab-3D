# Heltec V4 handheld controller

Find **Enclosures & devices → Handheld controllers → Heltec V4 handheld controller**, or search **Heltec** / **DS-225** / **power button**. This family uses the requested Heltec V4, two independently momentary controls and a compact power key opposite USB-C. All seven body and antenna configurations remain available. The requested operating frequency is 928 MHz; the antenna remains unselected, and the user measured the 102540 battery at 43 × 25 × 10 mm.

## Library selection

Choose the device family first, then a **Body model**, **Antenna type** and, for External SMA, an **External antenna position**. Body and antenna type are independent library filters. The body names describe the physical design:

| Body model    | Controls and grip                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Straight**  | Tapered body with two guarded side buttons for the index and middle fingers.                                                                      |
| **Palm**      | Rounded palm profile with the same two guarded side buttons.                                                                                      |
| **Palm Lock** | Palm profile with an index control and a recessed thumb-web second control, protected by a captive sliding safety with an adjustable metal clamp. |

**External SMA** uses the modeled pigtail mount. Its positions are **Bottom · USB end**, **Top · power end** and **Compact top · power end**. **Internal FPC** reserves an unselected adhesive antenna inside the lid and has no exterior SMA connector; this label does not select a purchased antenna.

## Assembly

1. Print the base floor-down, lid exterior-down and battery end clamp separately. Palm Lock also has a second-button plunger and sliding safety, for five prints in total. The Palm and Palm Lock base print layouts offset their additional lower contour onto the print bed. Use local removable support where needed at horizontal openings and nut pockets. The R4/R5 bottom arc may also need local perimeter support for a clean underside on the chosen material and layer height. Use removable local support under the safety gate, thumb-pad bridge, paddle-flange overhang and nut-pocket bridges as needed; their bearing surfaces control the sliding fit. Clear removable support beneath the base-owned fixed rail from its open USB-side end before threading the slider; the nominal access is 3.1 mm high and 21.4 mm deep. Deburr all sliding and bearing edges. For the battery-tower update, reprint the base and battery retainer together; the preceding PCB-seat lid still fits unchanged. Older lids predating the direct power-switch cradle also require replacement because the tail screw centers are X=±11.5 mm.
2. Insert six M2 nuts: four for the lid and two for the battery end clamp, before electronics obstruct access. Fit the selected lanyard cord while its anchor is accessible.
3. For an external-SMA option, loosely place the washer and retaining nut inside first, then feed the pigtail from outside through the selected D opening and hardware. Seat the flange fully in its hexagonal recess and D-key, then tighten the inner nut before electronics obstruct access. The Top SMA jack must be installed before the power switch and lid; tighten it with the actual axial nut driver, whose modeled outside clearance is Ø11 mm. Follow the compact mount’s actual tool-access and retention interface for that option. Check the local lead allowance and reserve the remaining flexible cable path. For the internal-FPC option, prepare the lid land for the selected adhesive and reserve the feed and lid-opening cable loop without covering the OLED mask or loading the LoRa U.FL socket.
4. On Straight and Palm, install both DS-225 buttons through the side wall, with index-finger B at Y=-35 mm and middle-finger A at Y=-10 mm. On Palm Lock, install the index control. Loosen the recessed DS-225 retaining hardware, lower its barrel into the open mounting slot, then tighten its retaining nut from inside before installing the paddle, slider or electronics. Fit the solid paddle in its guide. Fit the M3 nylon-insert nut in the slider pocket with the nylon collar facing inboard and the metal thread entrance facing the exterior screw. With the lid removed, thread the slider onto its rail from the open USB-side channel and move it to the unlocked end. The lid closes that channel with its thick end stop and captures the paddle. Fit the M3 washer and low button-head screw from outside, then adjust drag. Check released return, locked obstruction and full unlocked travel. The lid can reopen with the M3 clamp installed. Remove the clamp screw and washer only before withdrawing the slider from its rail. Insulate the DS-225 solder lugs and route the leads above the battery. Drop the user-owned tactile power switch into its open cradle; lid grooves and the broad top pad retain it. Temporarily close the lid to check free actuation before connecting GPIO0 and GND.
5. Fit the selected battery and floor pad, then secure the end clamp with two **M2 × 6** screws (M2 × 8/10 also fit) and fitted soft padding. Check pouch clearance and strain relief.
6. Guide the PCB front nose beneath the base wall and into the USB window, then lower its rear onto the edge supports. Check that the four relieved lid feet align with their PCB edge lands and clear GPIO pads, components and U.FL sockets on the delivered revision. Check OLED and connector clearance before connecting the battery.
7. Lower the lid so its relieved edge feet capture the PCB, then fit the alignment lip and four **M2 × 8** socket screws (M2 × 6/10/12 also fit the declared blind wells). Check USB insertion, power-switch return and guard clearance against a flat surface. For an internal antenna, leave enough free lead for opening the lid and keep the coax clear of screw posts and the display mask.
