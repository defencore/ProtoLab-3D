# Propeller and rotor

Twisted air and marine propellers, axial fans and centrifugal impeller forms.

Find **VEHICLE STRUCTURES → PROPELLERS & ROTORS → Propeller and rotor**.

## Variants

- Air propeller · 2 blades
- Air propeller · 3 blades
- Air propeller · 4 blades
- Marine propeller · 3 blades
- Axial impeller · 7 blades
- Centrifugal impeller · 12 vanes

## Controls

Rotor family; Rotor diameter; Hub diameter; Shaft bore; Hub height; Blade count; Root chord; Blade thickness ratio; Root blade angle; Tip blade angle.

## Scope

Four-point lens-like blade sections with geometric twist, not source NACA or a qualified propeller. No thrust, cavitation, fatigue, balancing or rotational-speed rating. Centrifugal variant uses straight radial vanes. Dimensions are editable prototype choices, not source-certified product dimensions or a manufacturing drawing. Threads are smooth nominal envelopes unless explicitly stated. No load, pressure or service-life rating is implied.

Separate named components are exported to FreeCAD; the exploded state separates them along Z. The preview and native export share the same shape recipe. This is an independent, editable package.
