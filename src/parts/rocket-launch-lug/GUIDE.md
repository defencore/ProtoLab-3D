# Rocket launch lug

One straight hollow guide sleeve corresponding to OpenRocket's [Launch Lug component](https://openrocket.readthedocs.io/en/latest/user_guide/basic_rocket_design.html#body-components-and-fin-sets). Z follows the guide rod; the origin is at the sleeve midpoint. Outside diameter, bore and length use millimetres. The same annulus defines preview, STL and native FreeCAD geometry.

The three presets are authored examples for nominal metric rods. They are not supplier parts or dimensional standards. Rod straightness, finish, actual clearance and attachment to the airframe must be measured and specified. A minimum positive wall is a geometric check, not a load qualification. No rod, mounting foot, adhesive or airframe is included.
