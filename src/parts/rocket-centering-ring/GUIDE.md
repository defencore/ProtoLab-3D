# Rocket centering ring

Single annular plate with a concentric through bore and square edges. Z is the thickness axis; the origin is at the plate midpoint. The bore and outside diameter are independent nominal design values. No fasteners, lightening holes, retention features or other assembly components are included.

## LDRS use

A reusable passive detail for high power hobby rocketry layouts and preparation for Large Dangerous Rocket Ships (LDRS). The package supplies a single physical component with editable millimetre dimensions.

## Presets and evidence

The four metric presets are author-defined prototype examples. Three are centering rings; the fourth is an annular stop matching the [OpenRocket Engine Block role](https://openrocket.readthedocs.io/en/latest/user_guide/basic_rocket_design.html#inner-components). Its shape does not establish thrust capacity or attachment strength. They are not manufacturer parts, measured products or published standard sizes, and intentionally carry no catalog evidence. Select actual tube and stock dimensions for the project instead of inferring commercial compatibility from a preset name.

## Geometry and export

Preview, binary STL and FreeCAD export describe the same nominal detail. The FreeCAD recipe creates one solid. Reported dimensions are the outside diameter on X/Y and the axial length or plate thickness on Z. The model has only the default physical state.

Prototype annular tube support; dimensions are author examples. Confirm both mating tube dimensions, material, fits and the structural attachment in the project.
