# Fuselage formers

This is one physical sheet former, laid flat in XY with stock thickness along Z. Round, elliptic and rectangular outlines support different fuselage sections. Four editable examples include a 100 mm round former, a 140 × 100 mm elliptic former, a 120 × 90 mm box former and an 80 mm solid round bulkhead. They are custom design starting points, without a commercial identity or a structural load rating.

The optional central service opening follows an ellipse for round and elliptic outlines and a rectangle for box outlines. Its width and height remain independently configurable. A round outside outline therefore can have an elliptic service opening. With a round outline, the configurator keeps outside height equal to width.

Four optional locating notches accept longitudinal stringers at the left, right, top and bottom. Finished notch width includes the chosen stock fit allowance. Notch depth is measured inward from the full width or height extremum, rather than from a nearby point on the curved outline. Validation rejects shallow cuts that do not enter the ellipse, notches that consume a side and service openings that remove the remaining web beneath a notch.

Four optional mounting holes are placed diagonally. Their centres lie at 45°, 135°, 225° and 315° on an ellipse whose semi-axes are the outside semi-axes minus the mounting inset. The same rule applies to box outlines. This is a configurable attachment layout, not a supplier mounting standard. Validation checks every hole against the outside contour, notches, service opening and other holes.

The outside and service ellipses are closed polygonal cutting contours; circular mounting holes remain analytic circles in native CAD and DXF. At the maximum 600 mm outside size the ellipse approximation has less than 0.011 mm nominal radial chord error. Preview, FreeCAD and DXF share the same outline and openings. The DXF is in millimetres and includes closed, unoffset paths. Apply tool compensation and cutting order in CAM.

Choose actual plywood, balsa or composite sheet and document grade, grain or laminate direction, measured thickness, adhesive, seat fits, edge finish and an inspection drawing. Use a stock-compatible cutting or milling process; rectangular seats need a square-corner cutting process or a defined corner-relief operation. Geometric minimum web does not establish structural capacity. Size former spacing, skins, stringers, joints and local attachments for the aircraft loads before production release or flight.
