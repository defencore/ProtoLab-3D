# BK12 / BF12 purchased bearing support

Primary source: [HIWIN B03TE03-1907](https://www.hiwin.com/wp-content/uploads/HIWIN-Ballscrew-Supports-Catalog.pdf), printed pages 4, 14–15, 23, 25–26 / PDF pages 7, 17–18, 26, 28–29. Source SHA-256: `4d5f2b51be24834bede8f7ce28942a56c8aeaa4c2685965855cb7d3554dc787f`.

Both supports have native screw axis X, shaft centre Y0/Z0, bottom Z−25, top Z18 and width 60 mm. Sourced dimensions include BK axial length 25, BF length 20, transverse mounting pitch 46, vertical drill diameter 6.6, counterbore 10.8×1.5 and frontal drill diameter 5.5 mm. BK has four vertical mounting holes at X±6.5/Y±23. The enlarged BF side view and photograph show one centred top bore per wing, at X0/Y±23; its generic drill-count leader nevertheless says four. The BF model follows the centred view and retains this conflict for supplier verification. Source frontal holes are at Y±23/Z0 or −18. The wing top is Z7.5.

BK12 has a 12 mm journal and two 7001A bearings; BF12 has a 10 mm journal and one 6000ZZ bearing. [NSK 7001A](https://www.nsk.com/au-en/engineering/7001a-sp-acbb-st-a.html) confirms 12×28×8 mm boundaries. [NSK 6000ZZ](https://www.nsk.com/ph-en/engineering/products/bearings/ball-bearings/deep-groove-ball-bearings/single-row-deep-groove-ball-bearings/6000zz-apn.html) confirms 10×26×8 mm, with seven balls of diameter 4.762 mm. The 7001 internal contact geometry remains representative.

HIWIN gives BK journal length 36, first 12 mm M12×1, two collars 12/15.5×5.5, and locknut D22/AF19×8. The BF journal projection is 11 mm, with source groove band 8–9.15 mm from the shoulder. [MISUMI JIS B2804](https://sg.misumi-ec.com/pdf/tech/mech/p2855.pdf) supplies the 10 mm shaft ring groove diameter 9.6 and width 1.15 mm. The installed S10 outline is illustrative. M12×1 and M4 remain nominal drawing callouts; female cutting-tool diameters 12.1 and 4.1 mm provide illustrative model clearance. `ThreadFitAllowance` records this difference in FreeCAD metadata; no production fit class is claimed.

`screwSupportParameters(model)`, `screwSupportLayout` and `supportMountingHoles` expose the interfaces. The reconstructed BK bearing-pair centre is X−0.5; collars occupy −14..−8.5 and 7.5..13, giving shoulder contact X13. The BF bearing is centred at X0 with shoulder contact X−4. These axial seat locations, race/ball contours, seal/lid details and lid-screw pattern are reconstruction assumptions. The model includes positive seat shoulders and a retaining lid.
