# FinsROV making details

The live catalog contains six confirmed FinsROV making definitions: the printed pressure-sensor mount, cable-passing screw, front cabin mount, horizontal thruster bracket, rear cabin hatch and thruster mount. Each is one connected physical solid. The assembly supplies the actual quantities and placements; this catalog is not a one-of-each build kit.

Print the pressure-sensor mount. The other five definitions are machined aluminium details: use the original CAD and machining workbook to establish stock, turning, milling, drilling, threads and finish. STL making export is disabled for these CNC details. Their FreeCAD macros and original STEP sources remain available.

Servos, thrusters, pressure sensors, pressure-cabin equipment and other complete purchased units are in **ROV purchased equipment**. Their internal CAD bodies are installation geometry and never making details in this catalog.

`lib/catalog.json` records each original file URL, revision, SHA-256, hardware license URL, material, manufacturing route and requirements. The **Original source and fabrication record** download preserves that evidence. Follow the selected project's source and license links for its editable originals and build instructions. See [the source notice](lib/NOTICE.md).

The six original STEP components retain native BREP surfaces and source-derived preview triangles without scaling or geometric redesign. The native CAD preserves the source interfaces; a triangulated display does not turn a machined metal part into a printable polymer design.

Manufacturing routes describe the selected physical detail. The active family has no laser-cut part or cutting process. Previously inspected Schillaci, MUR and LoCO source inventories remain research evidence outside this live making catalog; the prior file inventory must not be treated as an assemblable ROV.

Resolve fits, sealing faces, material, stock, finishing and assembly requirements from the selected source before fabrication. Leak, pressure, buoyancy and electrical tests belong to the complete vehicle; the geometry catalog assigns no new operating depth.
