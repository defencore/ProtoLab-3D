# ROV source geometry and attribution

The six live making definitions are original FinsROV source components. Source geometry belongs to the contributors identified in each `catalog.json` record. Each record provides the exact original file URL, source revision, SHA-256, hardware license and license URL. Those source links and license citations are also available in the catalog and source-record download.

Native data under `native/components.json` is a compressed geometry derivative of those six individually identified STEP files, retaining the source BREP surfaces. Source geometry is not scaled, welded, or redrawn. Display normals and colors are presentation only. The printed mount is the sole STL making export; the five metal details retain native CAD for machining.

Purchased equipment and its internal bodies are excluded from this family. The live source audit concerns these actual FinsROV making definitions. Earlier multi-project source inspections and rejected files are research records, not live fabricated product definitions or an assembly BOM.

The software loader does not replace or broaden a source hardware license. Preserve upstream authorship, applicable license terms and corresponding source obligations when redistributing a component or its geometry. Different projects remain separately licensed designs.
