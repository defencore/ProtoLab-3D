# Servo motor

This package contributes fifteen fixed supplier configurations to the grouped Servo motors selector: Waveshare ST3215-HS and ST3025; KST X10 Mini Pro-A and Pro-B, X10 V8.0, X10 Pro-A and Pro-B; Power-HD T60-BHV and TDS-2; Futaba HPS-H701; Hitec HSB-9380TH and D954SW; CHASERVO HV3609; and TowerPro MG996R / MG90S. The current grouped selector has nineteen choices including the separate bus, CNC-feed and QFS2025 catalogs. `presets.json` owns this package's catalog, source evidence and read-only search attributes. Numeric characteristics filter models; they are not editable geometry.

`catalogSelectionOnly` enables the manufactured-model picker. `lib/catalog-fields.ts` declares 16 `catalogFilterFields` for dimensions, torque, speed, current, voltage and construction. Numeric filters use inclusive bounds; selecting a result loads its stored model, while changing filters alone leaves the preview unchanged. Field summaries and measurement conditions come from catalog attributes, not geometry parameters.

The only public parameters are `model`, `outputAngle`, `showHorn`, and `hornStyle` (the ST3215 single arm or supplied disc pair). States are `assembled`, `exploded`, and `body`. Model changes constrain the output angle to the selected servo's geometric limit and reset disc selection outside ST3215. HPS-H701's ±90° limit is an illustrative inspection range because published travel is unavailable. Hitec limits use maximum programmable travel (HSB-9380TH ±80°, D954SW ±87.5°); their default ±60° travel requires programming before larger physical motion. Case, shaft and mounting dimensions are fixed per model. Unknown retired dimensional parameters fail validation.

Private `lib/catalog-models.json` records each fixed geometry input and source evidence, including approximations for undimensioned details. `lib/models.ts` dispatches to private ST3215 Waveshare, ST3025, KST and standard geometry families. Power-HD, Futaba, Hitec and CHASERVO use the standard family with separate fixed records. JavaScript meshes and FreeCAD shapes use the same dimensions and placements.

ST3215 has unequal front/rear mounting patterns and an optional supplied disc pair. ST3025 has its own installation envelope without projecting RC-servo ears. KST A/B retain their distinct side/flat mounting arrangements and 25-tooth shaft geometry. Standard models preserve their individual mounting centers: Power-HD 49 mm, Futaba and CHASERVO 49.5 mm, HSB-9380TH 49 mm and D954SW 48 mm, all with 10 mm row spacing. Undimensioned shafts and cutouts remain illustrative where identified by source evidence. Optional single arms are fixed illustrative accessories. These external installation models do not simulate internal mechanisms, torque or electrical operation.

Published torque and speed values are associated with a reference voltage. Current attributes carry their own test conditions, since the source may not state a voltage. Missing or ambiguous current values remain unknown and are excluded by a numeric current filter. All primary sources and discrepancies remain in the catalog specifications and geometry evidence.

Power-HD and Hitec numeric currents use the 7.4 V datasheet column. Both Waveshare models publish current without a test voltage; their 12 V torque/speed reference is not assigned to current. KST and Futaba have no numeric current ratings used here, CHASERVO has no published no-load current, and TDS-2 stall current is omitted because its V1 sheet uses the charge unit mAh. CHASERVO's torque filter uses its 36 kgf·cm holding figure at 7.4 V; its separately published output torque is 24 kgf·cm. `verifiedParameters: ['model']` preserves manufacturer identity after pose, horn or state changes without certifying illustrative geometry details.

CHASERVO's nominal 40 mm height is measured to the spline-root plane; its drawing verifies a 44.2 mm bare total height. Futaba, Hitec and ST3025 total heights remain unverified and are omitted from numeric search attributes. Current source discrepancies remain explicit: CHASERVO's product page lists 81 g and 500–2500 μs while its October 2024 PDF lists 70 g ±10% and 900–2100 μs; the catalog uses the current mass and the PDF's ±60° maximum travel. HSB-9380TH v2.2 specifies a 38.5 mm case and 0.18 s/60° at 6 V, correcting older 38 mm / 0.17 s listings. D954SW v2.2 specifies 66 g without a horn, versus 68 g on an older product page. Futaba's manufacturer sheet distinguishes rated 6–7.4 V from operating 4.8–8.4 V.

ST3215 uses `lib/waveshare/native.json`, baked from the unchanged manufacturer STEP by `scripts/import-st3215.py`. Eight native solids replace the former constructed casing/shaft/discs. Preview meshes and embedded compressed BREP share the same source and coordinates. A 0.00001 mm internal cavity repair makes the rear cover valid without changing external surfaces. The nonstandard output retaining thread is represented by a smooth D3.2 x8.4 bore, with an OEM thread callout requiring physical diameter/pitch verification. Its spline and gears remain unchanged. Original overlapping components are preserved; validation distinguishes those source overlaps from new interference. The default ST3215 selection shows both supplied discs.

## TowerPro assembly components

`servo-motor:towerpro-mg996r` and `servo-motor:towerpro-mg90s` make the TowerPro supplier identities used by Rocket Release and Rocket Airbrakes separately searchable. Model selection is fixed; assembly position and the three existing states remain available. Both models are explicitly installation references (`catalog.geometryEvidence.kind: envelope`). Source body dimensions conflict with the same manufacturer pages’ configure tables; those conflicts and all assumed ear, hole, shaft and horn geometry are recorded per model. No supplier-certified mounting pitch, output spline or full CAD reconstruction is claimed. The MG996R source states 0–159° travel, represented as centered ±79.5°; MG90S uses an illustrative ±90° inspection range. Current test voltage is unknown for MG996R; MG90S maximum operating voltage and current ratings remain unknown.

Verification for these added TowerPro selections: package contract checks pass with 15 presets. Thirteen generated TowerPro and Tattu 3S macro cases were executed in FreeCAD 1.1.4: all new TowerPro states with/without an optional horn and a 45° pose, plus the Tattu 3S exterior. Valid closed solids, preview/native bounds, analytic surface types and STEP solid round trips passed; three corresponding FCStd files were saved. The current combined report additionally covers both MA cylinder executions at three extensions and the three exact OMRON sensor selections: 22 cases and eight baseline STEP/FCStd round trips passed with identity retained. Reports and exports are under ignored `test-results/assembly-supplier-models/`. These checks establish model consistency, not missing supplier dimensions.
