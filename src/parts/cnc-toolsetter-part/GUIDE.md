# Flat toolsetter details

These are two independent fabrication details. The POM base is D50 x 8 mm and has a D40.2 mm locating recess 2 mm deep. The D40 x 10 mm aluminium contact seats at base Z6, giving nominal touch height 16 mm. Two ISO4762 M3 x 10 screws enter the contact counterbores (head seat Z7 in contact coordinates) and engage the POM threads. A radial M3 terminal hole accepts the conductive cable terminal; specify the purchased terminal screw and strain relief in the electrical handoff.

This removable flat plate has no mechanical overtravel. Calibrate its actual height, keep it electrically isolated from a metal table, qualify the probe input and remove it before machining. The source configurator establishes the flat/sprung option classes, not this own design's dimensions or accuracy. The sprung option needs a separate qualified mechanism and is not misrepresented by this flat geometry.
