# Rocket recovery shock cord

One purchased cord length represented by a straight circular strand. The family corresponds to Shock Cord in [OpenRocket's official recovery overview](https://openrocket.readthedocs.io/en/latest/user_guide/basic_rocket_design.html#mass-components), reviewed 2026-10-25. The source establishes the component type, not a material choice or dimensional recommendation.

Z follows the cord axis; the model is centered at the origin and ends at `+/-length / 2`. Preview, STL and FreeCAD describe the same nominal circular envelope. All three presets are author-defined references, without a supplier identity, minimum breaking strength or elongation specification.

Choose the actual cord and document routing, abrasion protection, end terminations, knot or sewn-loop allowances and harness attachment before procurement. The straight reference length does not establish the final cut length. No braid, stretch, elastic response, knot, loop or packed geometry is modeled. Verify the complete recovery load path with the actual components.
