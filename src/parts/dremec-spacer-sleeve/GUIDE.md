# DREMEC custom metal spacer sleeves

The four Ukrainian sleeve product families are made to customer dimensions. Manufacturer catalog page 36 specifies outside diameter 4–36 mm, body length 1–60 mm and a freely chosen bore. These ranges are not an enumeration of stock variants.

Choose steel/zinc, brass/nickel, aluminium or stainless steel, then enter the required bore, outside diameter and length. The steel default is the printed order example **324/2.1x10**, with 4 mm outside diameter. Other material templates are labeled customer-requested examples.

Supplier product links remain in the Sources section and in the exported component's SupplierProductSource property after dimensions change. Source material grade and finish are retained. Procurement requires confirming the requested execution and supplying a drawing; the browser does not place an order.

Both rendering modes keep an unthreaded, closed annular solid. Chamfers, tolerances and finish geometry are unspecified.

Sources: [official manufacturer catalog, PDF page 19 / printed page 36](https://shop.dremec.de/api/downloads/Dremec_Katalog_Deutsch_doppelseitig.pdf#page=19), [UA sleeves](https://dremec.ua/product-category/distanciyni-vtulki/). The complete source snapshot and coverage are in `src/catalog/dremec/`.
