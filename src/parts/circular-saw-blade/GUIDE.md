# Circular saw blades

Select the purchased Freud LU5H variant to match the spindle bore. Diameter, kerf, body thickness, tooth count and drive-hole pitch circles follow the linked manufacturer table. Tooth shape, gullet radii, brazing and pin-hole clocking are illustrative. The model must not be used to manufacture cutting tooling.

Use only the speed, feed direction and material range permitted by the delivered blade and the saw manufacturer. The modeled diameter does not establish a safe spindle speed.
