# Plain sleeve bushing

Cylindrical and flanged sliding bushings, with optional lubrication groove.

Find **BEARINGS & SEALS → PLAIN BEARINGS → Plain sleeve bushing**.

## Variants

- Sleeve bushing · Ø16 / Ø20 × 20 mm
- Flanged bushing · Ø16 bore

## Controls

Bushing form; Outside diameter; Shaft bore; Bearing length; Flange diameter; Flange thickness; Model detail.

## Scope

Dimensions are editable prototype choices, not source-certified product dimensions or a manufacturing drawing. Simple uses nominal envelopes for declared threads; Full models declared helical features. Features without a defined pitch/profile remain nominal in both modes. No load, pressure or service-life rating is implied.

Separate named components are exported to FreeCAD; the exploded state separates them along Z. The preview and native export share the same shape recipe. This is an independent, editable package.
