# CNC process cooling equipment

The spindle water loop and cutting coolant loop are separate purchased systems. The TEYU source publishes overall dimensions and hose-port diameter, not casing detail drawings. Contours within the reserved envelope are illustrative. CW3000 is a passive radiator; select from the actual heat load and ambient conditions, not spindle nameplate power alone.

CW-5000TITY is the explicit active alternative: single-phase 220-240 V, 50/60 Hz, nominal 750 W heat rejection, temperature stability +/-0.3 C and a 580 x 290 x 470 mm overall envelope. The TI pump is rated at 10 L/min and has a maximum pressure of 2.5 bar; these figures do not describe the same operating point. Its 8 L reservoir and 10 mm fast connectors remain separate from the cutting-coolant loop. The manufacturer model table is the source; no compressor internals or undocumented factory mounting holes are modeled.

Measure actual spindle flow, temperature and heat input after installing hoses. The selected JST source requires at least 5 L/min and qualified coolant/ambient temperature conditions. Confirm CW-5000 cooling performance at the actual setpoint and ambient temperature, provide the required service ventilation, and wire the delivered flow/temperature alarm to inhibit cutting. A nominal cooling capacity and stable tank sensor do not establish finished-part dimensional accuracy or eliminate spindle thermal growth. Obtain the delivered chiller manual, nameplate and alarm-contact details before release.

Source: [TEYU CW-5000 manufacturer model table](https://www.teyuchiller.com/cnc-router-spindle-water-chiller-cw5000.html).

The Vertex manufacturer-authored catalogue, hosted by its dealer, publishes the VWP-81-150K reservoir dimensions and immersion depth. Pump head dimensions and drilling pattern are not available in that source; the model labels its head as a reservation and cannot supply a verified pump mounting drawing. Request that drawing when ordering. The purchased kit includes the pump, reservoir, switch and hoses; do not add a second fabricated reservoir to the assembly BOM.

Provide chip filtering in the machine tray, drain slope and maintainable flexible lines. Enclosure access, fluid compatibility and electrical connections require production design release.
