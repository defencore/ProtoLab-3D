# Shaft coupling

Rigid, Oldham, bellows, slotted-beam and flange coupling forms.

Find **TRANSMISSION & LINKAGES → SHAFT COUPLINGS → Shaft coupling**.

## Variants

- Rigid coupling
- Oldham coupling
- Bellows coupling
- Beam coupling
- Flange coupling

## Controls

Coupling form; Outside diameter; Shaft bore; Overall length; Hub separation; Model detail. Controls for other construction types or absent components are hidden.

## Scope

Oldham grooves, beam slots and bellows are illustrative; select actual supplier dimensions before integration. Dimensions are editable prototype choices, not source-certified product dimensions or a manufacturing drawing. Simple uses nominal envelopes for declared threads; Full models declared helical features. Features without a defined pitch/profile remain nominal in both modes. No load, pressure or service-life rating is implied.

Separate physical components are exported to FreeCAD. The preview and native export share geometry definitions. This is an independent, editable prototype package; examples without catalog evidence are not manufactured product SKUs.
