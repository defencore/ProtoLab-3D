MF105 and MF106 share a 10 mm body diameter but are not interchangeable: their bores are 5 and 6 mm and their overall widths are 4 and 3 mm. MF105 fits the turned Ø5 TR8 journals. MF106 keeps the previously assigned Ø6 output shaft and the thinner output bearing stack. One common bearing type would require bore adapters or a redesigned output shaft, crank bores and retention stack.

All three listings were marked ready to ship when checked on 2026-10-29; confirm stock when ordering. The supplied F695ZZ-JVB Prom listing was unavailable, so the default uses MF105 without bore adapters. The SMB manufacturer drawings verify flange thicknesses; rendered internal geometry remains illustrative.

For the SMB set, finish the eight screw-seat pilots from 9.8 mm to their assigned Ø10 fit (nominal model diameter 10.02 mm). Keep the Ø5 screw journals, the two external retaining grooves at X=±19.8 and the original screw lengths. Screw bearing centres move from −17/+17 to **−16.8/+16.8 mm**, so the underside of each 0.8 mm flange still contacts the outer face of its 3 mm disc. The 3.2 mm non-flanged body projects 0.2 mm beyond the inner sheet face.

Reuse the same sixteen screw thrust-sleeve occurrences with revised turned dimensions: bore 5.02 mm, outside diameter 5.9 mm, **0.7 mm outer lengths and 1.8 mm inner lengths**. Their axial spans are −19.5..−18.8, −14.8..−13, 13..14.8 and 18.8..19.5 mm. The 5.9 mm abutment is a preliminary inner-ring contact dimension; confirm the ordered SMB ring face before production. The two inner screw rings and their grooves are omitted. Each inner sleeve seats on the square thread-root shoulder at X=±13; keep the continuous nominal Ø5.5 root-core annulus intact at that face. Only two outer rings per screw remain: eight screw rings per block instead of sixteen. The block has 115 component occurrences instead of 123. No new pieces or bore adapters are introduced. Set the final sleeve lengths to provide assigned axial end float, avoiding unintended bearing preload. Qualify the root-shoulder contact area and actual thrust load.

MF106 and MF63 retain the old installed envelopes, bearing centres, output journals, guide seats and output sleeves. Their material changes from the former stainless EZO execution to SMB chrome bearing steel. The small inner bearings remain a separate size because a Ø10 bearing would require a new central guide and shaft design; it cannot be substituted in the existing Ø6 guide bore.

The lower load ratings of the smaller MF105 must be checked against actual screw thrust. Native geometric clearance checks do not establish actuator load capacity, bearing life, rigidity or production fits.

# TR8x8 twin-pin mini laser block, diameter 54 mm

Search for **TR8 twin pin mini**. The approved diameter 64 mm model and all previous variants remain unchanged. This version has a 131 x 54 x 54 mm bounding size with the 361:1 motor, three diameter 54 mm discs, and a coaxial 5 mm passage through the whole assembly.

## Compact output stack

The outward shafts and bearing supports move inward by 5 mm. Finished shaft length is 29.95 mm, outward end Y=22.5, outer bearing centre Y=12, clip centre Y=14.4, and output-flat start Y=16.3 mm. The inner-race sleeve shortens from 5.95 to 0.95 mm; the bearing span is 17.7 mm. The inner journal, bearing centre, monolithic crank and twin-trunnion brass nut stay as approved. Screw axes remain at radius 16 mm; motor, 50 mm screw and 30 mm column stations retain their axial positions.

Three laser discs are 54 x 3 mm. Four column lines lie at radius 23.5 mm and clocks 18, 108, 198 and 288 degrees. Moving the columns between supports avoids interference with the compact outer bearing cheeks. Purchased column bodies and 5 mm M3 engagements stay unchanged. The accepted crank supplies a containing-diameter lower bound of 53.5463 mm; 54 mm is the smallest whole-millimetre disc for that retained geometry. Output tails and retaining hardware intentionally extend along the four X directions beyond the circular rim.

## Centre passage and guide walls

Every disc has a central diameter 5 mm bore. Four guide walls use 2 mm steel with their original contact face at local Y=-5.05 mm, so the nuts, follower slots and bearing centres stay fixed. The 1.9 mm body behind the SMF63ZZ flange seats in the 2 mm guide. Each guide is one flat laser part; no milling, folded bracket or laminated wall is introduced.

Guide height increases from 18.7 to 21.45 mm (Z=-5.25..16.2), maintaining approximately the original gross in-plane section inertia despite the reduced thickness. This section comparison does not establish the actual slotted-wall stiffness or load rating. End keys move to local Z=-2.75 mm and fit 2.1 x 3.1 mm disc slots, leaving more than 3 mm of material around the central bore. Finish and align the bearing seats, key fits and pin slots; review actual guide loads, axial bearing reactions, key stress, stiffness and wear.

## Lightened and solid-web discs

The default lightened option adds four round windows per disc, halfway between screw axes. Motor-disc windows are diameter 7.4 mm on radius 18.5 mm; fixed/end-disc windows are diameter 5.4 mm on radius 16 mm. Existing bearing seats, column bolt zones, receiver slots and outer rim material remain. Each new window has at least 3 mm nominal clearance from neighbouring finished openings and edges.

Side windows remove about 1065.75 cubic millimetres across three discs, or 8.37 g at an assumed steel density of 7.85 g per cubic centimetre. Centre bores remove a further 176.71 cubic millimetres, about 1.39 g. Combined disc savings are about 9.75 g relative to discs with no lightening windows or centre bore. These are geometric mass estimates.

The **Disc construction** selector retains a solid-web option with the same diameter 5 mm centre passage. Separate prefixes and manifests keep the two cutting sets distinct. There are 15 laser blanks: eleven in 3 mm stock (three discs, four outer cheeks and four cranks) and four guide walls in 2 mm stock. Six unique DXF/SVG contours define each construction.

## Manufacturing

Use the selected current disc/guide files together. Finish bearing pilots, crank bores and matched 2 mm pin joints. Inspect compact edge ligaments, final shaft end float, output-attachment clearance and the full independent +/-20 degree motion. The custom 8 mm brass nut engagement, trunnions, 3 mm inner journals and source bearing internals retain their original load and manufacturing qualifications. Nominal nut-to-shaft clearance remains 0.5 mm. Frame stiffness must be assessed under the actual fastening and bearing loads before manufacture.
