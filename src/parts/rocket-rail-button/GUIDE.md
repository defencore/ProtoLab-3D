# Rocket rail guide button

One configurable turned polymer body for LDRS hobby rocket layout. The two flanges, groove root, through bore and flat-bottom screw-head recess use one revolved profile for preview and native FreeCAD export. Dimensions are in millimetres; Z is the screw axis and the mounting face is at `-height / 2`.

## Presets and evidence

The three presets are prototype examples. The small D10 x 9.7 and large D16 x 11 envelopes use the rounded metric outside dimensions in [Apogee's rail-button comparison](https://apogeerockets.com/building_supplies/rail_buttons/1in_1010_rail_button_standard), reviewed 2026-10-25. The source's rounded inch values are not exact equivalents. Groove dimensions, flange thickness, screw clearance and recess dimensions are authored examples. No verified supplier identity, rail fit, material grade or load rating is assigned. The custom D14 x 12 example has no supplier dimension claim.

## Interfaces

Measure rail slot width, channel width and depth, running clearances and fastener head before choosing dimensions. The validation retains positive flange overhang, groove width, bore wall and head-seat material; those checks establish geometric feasibility only. No mounting screw, backing nut or airframe reinforcement is included. Add each mounting item independently to the project and choose the quantity of buttons there.

Specify material, tolerances, surface finish, screw retention and attachment loads on the released drawing. Test travel over the full actual rail and qualify the attachment with the actual airframe.
