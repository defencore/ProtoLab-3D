# ProtoLab CNC / 800x1500x200

This is an independently dimensioned, bolted ProtoLab design with 800 x 1500 x 200 mm travel. Its library ID is `cnc-milling-machine`. The supplier page is a research reference for work range and equipment classes. See the [source review](../../../docs/cnc/design-and-source-review.md), [mechanical qualification](../../../docs/cnc/mechanical-qualification.md) and [precision drive selection](../../../docs/cnc/precision-drive-selection.md) for this design's geometry, component choices and pending qualification.

The intended use is D16T/duralumin and other nonferrous-metal prototypes, including aerospace MVP development. The required complete finished-part tolerance field is **0.05 mm, or ±0.025 mm**. A provisional 100 N finishing load is the design case; 300 N is a sensitivity case. This target has not been demonstrated on a built machine. Steel needs a separately accepted light-cut process with a suitable cutter, spindle torque and measured force; a low feed alone does not qualify it.

Two steel 300 x 100 x 10 mm bed members, separate lower flanges and six rounded 100 x 100 x 8 mm steel cross-ribs support the fixed aluminium T-slot table. The moving portal uses a 1375 mm steel box beam on two short, rounded 100 x 300 x 8 mm steel posts. Each 214 mm post seats between separate 25 mm aluminium caps and a 160 mm-wide horizontal foot. Cut-to-length M10 threaded stock and class8 nuts clamp the stack; separate beam bolts are reached through actual side ports that retain a continuous upper ring. Two one-piece aluminium T links connect the feet to the under-table Y tiebar. A short 40 x 400 x 200 mm left end plate retains the X drive interface. These replace the former tall side slabs; the central Y screw stays below the table and tray. X has a rear drive and two bridges around the stationary beam. Each axis has two profile rails and four carriages.

The current optimization narrows unused inner foot stock and adds two real milled capsule openings to the X end plate and beam insert. Steel beam, post-wall and tiebar sections remain intact; the shorter posts fit thicker caps. The cut M10 ties avoid special long smooth-shank bolts while preserving a stronger axial-stiffness screening case. Actual native volume comparisons and their exact scopes are retained under `test-results/cnc-milling/optimization/portal/`. They exclude replacement hardware and are not measured machine mass or assembled-joint qualification. Cap bending, actual stock certificates, preload, beam-wall thread strength, locking, joint slip and post-port stress still require engineering release.

The compact Z head has a **380 mm-wide integral U tower** with a 20 mm web and 12 x 80 mm fins. Its bottom stays 220 mm above the table. A separate 226 mm-wide moving U saddle has a 30 mm web, retained 80 mm fins, a central nut boss and machined rear pockets. End stock is shortened: its top is tableTop+545 mm and its bottom is clampLow+62 mm. The maximum-limit flag and sensor follow the shortened plate; the actual stroke remains200 mm. The default saddle falls from7.8917 to6.6398 kg by native volume at assumed2700 kg/m³; this is a15.86% saddle reduction, not a weighing of the complete Z axis. Actual guide/clamp slices retain their net section; relocated M4 flag holes make a disclosed local upper-slice change of up to0.66%. The Z screw is centered at X=0, Y=178 mm; actual BK15/BF15 supports bolt directly through 5 mm steel shims to the fixed web. There is no separate side nut arm or remote lower support platform. These are one-piece machined blanks, not plates with decorative unjoined ribs.

The selected purchased feed sets are HIWIN FDI 25 x 10 on X/Y and 20 x 5 on Z, with C3 accuracy and requested preload awaiting delivered certificates. Delta 750 W servos include a factory-braked Z execution; R+W couplings and HEIDENHAIN linear feedback retain their own source interfaces. Each physical leaf retains a library ID, configuration, manufacturing or procurement record and canonical occurrence ID. Matched screw/nut sets remain one purchasing order with two installed physical children.

Tool projection from the spindle nose is adjustable from 20 to 50 mm, default 25 mm. Its reference tip spans the table surface to 200 mm above it. A 200 mm stock slab fits the fixed-head envelope but leaves no upward cutter retract at the top of the stroke. Allow at most 180 mm stock for 20 mm retract before fixture and holder allowances. Projection is a geometric setup assumption until an actual cutter and collet are qualified; a long flexible cutter can consume the entire dimensional tolerance.

Use Assembly for the midpoint installation and the named X/Y/Z minimum or maximum states for endpoint inspection. Z assembly / details exposes the compact head. Inspection separation moves complete fixed, gantry, X and Z groups along Y with a 100 mm gap between their conservative posed source envelopes. The fixed bed, scales, sensors and equipment remain together, and each fastener stays with its host. Inspection, mechanism and bed views retain the canonical BOM. Continuous paths and driven internal-joint animation are outside these states. Simple and Full export the same physical configuration; Full adds implemented thread flanks and source-qualified detail, not an accuracy certificate.

To manufacture the frame, open each actual library configuration and release its drawing: material certificate, blank allowance, datums, fits, surface finish, thread strength, preload, assembly order and inspection. Purchased supplier internals are opaque units, not home-manufacturing instructions. Bounded portal tests verify actual bearing-face contact, bolt engagement and tip reserves, side-port wrench access, measured net post sections and the recorded endpoint clearances in Simple and Full. These checks do not establish assembled stiffness or dimensional accuracy. Long-bed datum machining, foundation/leveling, guide/screw alignment, chip protection, brake behavior, coolant flow/temperature, wiring, guards and loaded machining acceptance remain necessary before manufacture and powered operation. Current geometric tests establish only their recorded finite scope.

Native CAD leaves carry their actual `ManufacturingJSON` or `ProcurementJSON` records.
