# Slewing ring bearing

Two independently movable annular rings with mounting circles and independent mounting bolt circles.

Find **BEARINGS & SEALS → ROTARY TABLES → Slewing ring bearing**.

## Variants

- Slewing bearing · Ø150 / Ø90 mm
- Slewing bearing · Ø250 / Ø160 mm

## Controls

Outside diameter; Centre opening; Bearing height; Holes per bolt circle; Mounting hole diameter.

## Scope

Ring envelopes and bores only; no rolling-element raceway, preload or load rating. Dimensions are editable prototype choices, not source-certified product dimensions or a manufacturing drawing. Threads are smooth nominal envelopes unless explicitly stated. No load, pressure or service-life rating is implied.

Separate named components are exported to FreeCAD; the exploded state separates them along Z. The preview and native export share the same shape recipe. This is an independent, editable package.
