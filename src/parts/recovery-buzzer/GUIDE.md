# Recovery buzzer JHE20B

Self-powered finder buzzer with battery, sound outlet, connector, LED and button.

Body envelope: 20 x10 x8 mm. Loose leads excluded.
Separate PCB, housings, terminals and components are reconstructed with simple
solids. The recovery assembly uses this same library geometry. Mounting and
electrical connections must be verified on the purchased hardware.

The overall dimensions and appearance come from the user-supplied photograph. Component positions, package sizes, sleeve shape and contact arrangement are illustrative, not measured PCB fabrication data.
