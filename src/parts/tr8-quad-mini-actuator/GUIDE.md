# TR8x8 mini four-drive block

This is a separate compact variant. The original 82 mm four-drive mechanism, single-drive TR8 and SFK variants remain available. Search for **TR8 mini** in the library. Four independent outward 6 mm shafts form an X at one axial station. Their travel remains -20 to +20 degrees.

## Compact arrangement

Three round steel discs are 72 mm diameter and 3 mm thick. The four channel origins lie at radius 20.25 mm and angles 45, 135, 225 and 315 degrees. The output pivot remains X=0. The screw axis is 14.5 mm from the output axis. Main-disc starts are X=-52, -19 and 16 mm; the original standard 30 mm female/female and male/female M3 columns, 2 mm stage shims and M3x8 bolts remain. Column lines move to radius 28 mm at 25 degrees plus quarter turns.

All laser parts use one 3 mm steel sheet thickness: three main discs, eight output-bearing cheeks and eight pinned forks. The four inner cheeks interleave clockwise with 0.2 mm seams. Their slots and rails stay centred at output-shaft height. Receiver slots are 3.10 by 3.10 mm. Confirm actual sheet thickness, cut allowance and tab fit before cutting.

## Smaller output bearings

Eight **EZO SMF106ZZ** stainless, metal-shielded bearings replace the larger F686 output bearings. Each is 6x10x3 mm, with an 11.2 mm flange diameter and a 0.6 mm flange. Dimensions and the 6.8-6.9 mm inner-ring abutment range follow the [manufacturer table](https://www.ezo-brg.co.jp/english/product/spec.php?eid=00582). The purchased bearing envelope and abutments are verified; rendered raceways, cages and ball dimensions remain illustrative. Supplier bearing ratings do not establish the complete actuator's load capacity.

Output-bearing centres are Y=-12 and +12 mm. Their 2.4 mm bodies sit in plain 3 mm cheeks; the extra 0.6 mm bore depth clears the adjacent forks by 0.35 mm nominally. Finish eight cheek pilots from 9.8 mm to the assigned bearing fit (model 10.02 mm). Two F695 screw bearings per channel remain unchanged; finish the eight main-disc pilots from 12.8 mm to their assigned fits (model 13.02 mm).

Thrust spacers have a 6.05 mm bore and a **6.85 mm outside diameter**, within EZO's abutment range. Inner spacers are 0.95 mm long and outer spacers 0.5 mm. Do not reuse the larger original-version spacers against the SMF106 shields. The model's 6.05 mm spacer bore permits 0.025 mm radial offset on a nominal 6 mm shaft; at full offset, the 6.85 mm abutment spans the manufacturer's 6.8-6.9 mm limits. Assign the actual concentric fit and OD tolerances together, with an edge fillet no larger than 0.1 mm; the nominal clearance geometry is not a tolerance specification.

## Linkage and shafts

The purchased brass H nut remains 22x11x15 mm and is clocked 90 degrees. Its machined carrier narrows to 13 mm overall, preserving 1.4 mm nominal wall outside its 10.2 mm bore. The 3 mm steel forks retain unsplit 10 mm hubs, closed 4.1 mm slots and two ISO 8734 2x10 mm cross pins per shaft. Their arms are 8.5 mm wide with 4.25 mm end radii, retaining 2.2 mm slot walls. Finish 5.8 mm shaft pilots to 6.05 mm; match-drill and ream transverse 2 mm holes after setting sleeve and sliding clearances. The slots constrain carrier roll while allowing the pins to slide radially.

Each output shaft is 43.5 mm long, from Y=-16 to +27.5 mm. Its integral 7.5x1 mm inner head spans -16 to -15 mm. Outer retaining rings lie at +/-14.4 mm in 0.8 mm wide, 5.7 mm root-diameter grooves; the head has 0.25 mm nominal ring clearance. The longer outward end retains the original D-flat from 21.3 to 27.5 mm. The drilling and turning JSON gives the actual current stations.

## Screw and motion

The motor and 3x4 jaw coupling are unchanged. Cut each 250 mm TR8x8(P2) stock and turn it to 52 mm finished length with 28 mm working thread. Four starts, 2 mm pitch and 8 mm lead are preserved. Nut travel is `14.5*tan(angle)` mm: +/-5.277568 mm, or 10.555137 mm total. The full nut remains on the working thread with 1.2224 mm nominal margin at either end. All four commands are independent; the main frame stays fixed.

## Fabrication files

Use only files prefixed **TR8-quad-mini** for this version. DXF/SVG profiles are millimetres at 1:1 scale. The set includes all three main discs, eight bearing cheeks, one fork profile for quantity eight, a native blank macro, component list, motion table and machining schedules. Main and output bearing pilots differ in diameter. The transverse fork holes are machining operations, not through-laser features. Deburr, finish seats and assign fits, runout, material grades, groove tolerances and actual application loads before manufacture.
