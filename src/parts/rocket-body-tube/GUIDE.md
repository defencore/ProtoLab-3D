# Rocket body tube

Straight open tube with square ends and constant wall thickness. The longitudinal axis is Z; the origin is at the tube midpoint. The bore is outside diameter minus twice the wall thickness. No slots, holes, joints or fittings are included.

## LDRS use

A reusable passive detail for high power hobby rocketry layouts and preparation for Large Dangerous Rocket Ships (LDRS). The package supplies a single physical component with editable millimetre dimensions.

## Presets and evidence

The five metric presets are author-defined prototype examples. Three describe airframe tubes, one describes an internal tube and one describes a single tube fin, following [OpenRocket's component roles](https://openrocket.readthedocs.io/en/latest/user_guide/basic_rocket_design.html#available-design-elements). Use separate project occurrences for a tube-fin set. They are not manufacturer parts, measured products or published standard sizes, and intentionally carry no catalog evidence. Select actual tube and stock dimensions for the project instead of inferring commercial compatibility from a preset name.

## Geometry and export

Preview, binary STL and FreeCAD export describe the same nominal detail. The FreeCAD recipe creates one solid. Reported dimensions are the outside diameter on X/Y and the axial length or plate thickness on Z. The model has only the default physical state.

Prototype airframe tube; nominal dimensions are author examples. Material, tube tolerances, joint design and structural suitability require project-specific definition.
