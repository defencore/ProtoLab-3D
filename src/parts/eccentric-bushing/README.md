# Miniature eccentric bushing

MISUMI ECCB hex-head bushings for adjustable miniature bearing seats.

Find **BEARINGS & SEALS → ECCENTRICS & FOLLOWERS → Miniature eccentric bushing**.

Nominal MISUMI ECCB dimensions: D bearing seat, F seat length, V plain through bore, B hex flats, T head thickness and e eccentricity. Total length is F + T. Matching bearing designations are shown in the presets; bearings are not included. Edge breaks and tolerance bands are not modelled. Changing a dimension creates a custom bushing.

Preview and FreeCAD export share a solid geometry recipe. Every physical component exports separately; eccentric orientation changes the actual bore or roller position. Catalog presets remain editable. Sources are external links; no reference PDFs are bundled.

- [MISUMI · Eccentric Bushings ECCB · p. 1155](https://es.misumi-ec.com/pdf/fa/2014/P1_1155-1156_F16_EN.pdf)
