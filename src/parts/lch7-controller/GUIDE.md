# LCH7 v3.2

Controller assembly with an FR4 PCB, mounting holes, major components, connector
housings and contacts. Body envelope: 50.97594 x44 x7.48268 mm, excluding loose
leads. PCB: 50 x44 x1.12268 mm; four D4 holes on 30.6 mm square pitch.

Support the board on silicone grommets and metal compression limiters. In the
CO2 4S assembly, use the interchangeable POM-C D62 x6 carrier: turned faces,
round drilled ports and four M2.5x0.45 through-tapped mounting holes. ISO 4762
M2.5x20 screws enter from the PCB side, with no nuts on the board. Avoid direct
metal contact with the PCB and verify the tightening torque and plastic thread
retention. Rigid plates are machined from solid stock; shaped battery insulators
are separate unfilled PA12 printed parts.

The recovery assembly uses this same component model. Connector openings and
small contacts are simplified; verify fit and cable access on purchased hardware.
Input voltage, current, mass and pinout are not specified. Confirm supply
regulation before connecting a 4S pack at 16.8 V.
