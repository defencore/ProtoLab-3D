# Rocket preparation V cradle

One continuous flat-bottom V support for horizontal ground preparation and display at hobby rocket events such as Large Dangerous Rocket Ships (LDRS). Add two independent occurrences to a project when a pair is required. A single library item always represents one cradle.

## Geometry and coordinates

X is transverse to the rocket, Y follows its resting axis and Z is vertical. The origin lies at the center of the flat lower bench face. The V is a straight-sided notch running through the entire support depth. Its apex retains the selected `floor` thickness, and its upper edges retain at least 3 mm of material on either side.

The plate-like prism is one connected physical solid. Preview and FreeCAD share the same seven-vertex cross-section. Dimensions are in millimetres. The included V angle is calculated from the opening and the height above the apex; the opening is not a certified matching tube diameter.

All three examples are authored prototype dimensions, without a supplier source or load rating. Choose the opening and support height for the actual tube contact geometry and any padding. This support has no straps, anti-slip feet, padding or attachment holes.

## Intended use

This is a bench preparation and display support. Define the material and fabrication route, support spacing, padding and independent body restraint, and verify the bench and supported body's stability. The dimensional validation only preserves a connected printable or machinable shape; it does not qualify loading or contact pressure.
