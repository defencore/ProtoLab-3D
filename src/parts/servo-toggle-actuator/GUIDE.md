# Servo toggle actuator

Find **Electronics → Switches & buttons → Servo toggle actuator**. The default **Printed rocker** mounts a standard ST3215 12 V bus servo and a bare DAIER ASW-07D using three printed parts. **Parallel-shaft rocker** provides a narrower arrangement with a horizontal rotor and the servo shaft parallel to the switch bushing. **Guided fork** retains the larger assembly with guide rods and soft contact pads. All three use the purchased servo and switch library units.

## Operation

Select **OFF → Press ON → ON → Press OFF → OFF** to inspect a complete cycle. At either rest stage the servo is centred and the lever has clearance. A press stage moves a rounded finger or soft pad against one side of the lever. The mechanism returns to centre while the maintained toggle remains on or off.

The stages show static contact positions. Measure the delivered switch's pivot, force and trip positions before calibrating powered motion. The displayed endpoints do not program the servo or establish a force limit.

## Printed rocker

Print these three parts in qualified PETG or PA:

1. **Integrated base, servo cradle and switch mount.** The cradle and front reaction wall support the servo case. The switch fits the keyed mounting panel in the same printed body.
2. **Rear retaining clamp.** Two horizontal M3×45 through-bolts and captive M3 nuts retain it against the servo. Fit the printed contact surfaces to the delivered case and verify free servo motion after tightening.
3. **Two-finger rocker.** Its Ø26 × 4 mm hub attaches to the supplied disc with four M1.6×8 through-fasteners and AF3.2 × 1.3 mm nuts on the source Ø14 mm pitch circle. The two rounded fingers push opposite sides of the lever and leave it free at centre.

The default footprint is 100 × 90 mm and the switch case mounting face is 42 mm above the base bottom. The 3 mm printed switch panel occupies Z=45–48 mm between the switch's M12 retaining nuts. Account for print shrinkage and clear the keyed Ø12.2 mm opening before tightening the switch. The raised switch panel spans about 38 mm; provide printing supports beneath it and remove them before assembly. Print orientation, supports and material need qualification on the actual printer.

At the default height, the lowest switch blade is at Z=12.7 mm above the 6 mm mounting floor, leaving 6.7 mm below the metal blade. Select **Switch height** to suit the purchased 6.3 mm receptacles, insulation boots and cable bend. The boots and wires are not modeled; clearance to the source metal blade does not establish clearance for every connector or boot. Raising the switch height also raises the servo and rocker while preserving their contact geometry.

The servo's output axis is horizontal, along Y. Its supplied disc face is at Y=18 mm and the printed hub occupies Y=14–18 mm. The rounded fingers extend forward to the lever around Y=0, leaving manual access from above. Low lips at both cradle ends rise approximately 2.36 mm above the servo case bottom, leaving the case ends open for cable access. The servo case is retained externally; its OEM pilot holes carry no mounting screws.

The fixed fingers have 3 mm tip radii, with centres at X=±10.3 mm and 12 mm above the modeled switch pivot. Their clear opening at rest is 14.6 mm, giving approximately 1.08 mm normal clearance to either stable lever position. The contact angle is calculated from the circular finger and Ø7 mm lever geometry, approximately 28.37° from centre. Base dimensions and switch height change the mounting arrangement while preserving this contact geometry. The contact-height, opening and sweep controls belong to **Guided fork**.

Rounded fingers contact the lever without gripping it. Calibrate actual trip positions, speed, acceleration and torque limits against the purchased switch. Return the rocker to centre after each push. Printing and geometric contact alone do not establish the available force, fatigue life or resistance to creep.

## Parallel-shaft rocker

Select **Parallel-shaft rocker** for a PETG unit mounted at the toggle panel level. The default mounting flange is 80 × 110 × 6 mm, centred at Y=−27 mm. Its flat underside is the installation datum Z=0 and sits on the top of the host plate. The host plate occupies Z=−t…0, where t is its actual thickness. Two separate 0.8 mm TPU frames sit at the switch-case and host-plate interfaces, giving six prints in total. The flange has R4 outer corners; the housing and lid use R3 outer corners. Five-millimetre walls and six upper gussets reinforce the housing above the flat seating plane.

The open-bottom lower cradle spans Z=−19.7…0 mm. Its switch register has a 38 × 26 mm outer profile with R3 corners and a fitted 28.8 × 16.8 mm cavity, extending the servo pocket to the switch-body flats to resist rotation. The upper channel clears the horizontal ground terminal, and the lower opening flares for the vertical receptacles. Local rear ledges retain the servo while preserving access from below. Four Ø8 × 1 mm collars locate the cradle in Ø8.4 mm upper recesses at the flat Z=0 joint. Fit these features to the delivered components and printed dimensions.

Four M3×30 through-bolts join the lid, upper flange and lower cradle, engaging nuts at Z=−14…−11.6 mm in the lower part. They do not use the servo's OEM pilot holes. Tighten progressively against the fitted housing stops and verify free servo motion; the switch-body register and keyed bushing provide mechanical location without relying on TPU friction.

Cut the through-opening in the host plate from the matching template. Its main profile is a 56 × 84 mm rounded rectangle with R4 corners, spanning X=−28…28 mm and Y=−70…14 mm. A joined left branch spans X=−42…−26 mm and Y=−6…6 mm, with R2 outer corners, for the horizontal ground-terminal receptacle and insertion access. It extends 2 mm beyond the left edge of the default 80 mm flange. Four round Ø4.5 mm mounting holes take M4 hardware; on the default flange their centres are X=±32 mm and Y=−74 mm or Y=20 mm. Use `panel-cutout-template.dxf` or the SVG template at 100% scale, checking its 50 mm line before cutting. The grey carrier outline and axes are references only. A changed flange footprint needs the matching mounting-hole pattern: X=±(L/2−8), Y=−27±(W/2−8). The **Larger flange** example is 100 × 120 mm. The separate installation STEP/FCStd files add a reference 3 mm host plate.

The switch case datum is Z=0. A lower Ø21 mm relief occupies Z=0…3 mm, leaving the keyed 3 mm switch mounting web at Z=3…6 mm. The source lower nut fits inside this relief and the upper nut remains at Z=6…8.4 mm. Thus the printed flange is 6 mm overall while the source switch still clamps its verified 3 mm installation stack. The switch body and blades project below the host plate; leave access for the selected 6.3 mm receptacles, insulation boots and cable bends.

The switch TPU frame, `parallel-switch-gasket`, is 32 × 24 mm with R2 outer corners and a Ø21.2 mm centre opening. It seats beneath the case's end regions, outside the rigid M12 stack, in a 32.4 × 24.4 mm recess. The host-plate TPU frame, `parallel-panel-gasket`, has a 78 × 108 mm R3 outer profile centred at Y=−27 mm, with a 56.8 × 84.8 mm R4.4 opening centred at Y=−28 mm. Its left clearance spans X=−42.4…−25.6 mm and Y=−6.4…6.4 mm, leaving one connected C-shaped frame around the terminal opening. Four Ø12 clearances leave the rigid Ø11 mm PETG lands around the M4 holes exposed at Z=0. Both frames are nominally 0.8 mm thick and sit flush in matching 0.8 mm recesses at Z=0…0.8 mm. The model applies no compression; the hard mounting lands and unchanged switch web preserve alignment. The gaskets have no assigned sealing or compression-force rating.

The underside remains open for the servo control lead. An authored 45 × 18 mm approach corridor provides access from below. Reserve at least 25 mm below the servo face for the selected plug and wire bend. The supplier CAD does not include the actual ports, mating plugs or connected cables, so verify their positions and fit on the delivered servo before final mounting. The horizontal switch terminal has a proposed 17 × 9 × 5 mm receptacle space at X=−31.5…−14.5 mm, Y=−4.5…4.5 mm and Z=−5.1…−0.1 mm, with a 10 mm insertion allowance from the left. Measure the chosen receptacle and insulation boot; those accessories are not modeled or verified. There is no spanning floor beneath the switch or servo, and **Switch height** is hidden for this mechanism.

Use PETG for the four rigid parts. Approximately five to six walls are a starting print setup, subject to physical qualification. Print the lower cradle with its coplanar open rim at Z=−19.7 mm down. Print the upper flange with its underside Z=0 down, leaving only the shallow gasket and locating recesses to bridge. This split greatly reduces support requirements; verify bridges and add local removable supports where the printer needs them. Clear the gasket seats, collar recesses, nut entries and terminal channels, and keep the mating planes flat. Print the lid's broad upper face down with its contact ribs upward, the rotor's flat disc mating face down, and both TPU frames flat. Finish the rounded contact ends smoothly. Material choice, wall count and gussets do not establish a strength or service-life rating.

The rotor is a straight bar with two opposite arms centred on the servo shaft. Its capsule centreline runs from X=−28 to X=+28 mm at neutral, along Y=−28 mm and 10 mm above the modeled switch pivot. The rounded body has a 5 mm radius, clipped to an 8 mm thickness with flat upper and lower faces. Its Ø26 × 8 mm hub attaches to the unchanged supplied disc on a Ø14 mm pitch circle using four M1.6×12 screws and AF3.2 × 1.3 mm nuts. Align the nut flats radially to clear the source hub and retain the supplied shaft fastener.

The servo's output shaft and switch bushing follow Z; the neutral lever's nominal axis is parallel to them. The servo shaft lies 28 mm behind the switch, with the case extending away along negative Y. Its native datum is Z=−15.2 mm; the rotor occupies Z=18.5…26.5 mm. The lid's Ø21 mm opening clears the source Ø19.2 mm disc, with a nominal 1 mm gap beneath the rotor, including the recessed screws. Verify these clearances after printing.

With the bar centred across the servo, the complete −12° to +12° manual lever path stays clear. Conservative surface clearance is at least 11.5 mm to the central hub and 19.5 mm to the bar. You can switch **OFF by hand without moving the servo** at neutral; the bar does not surround or grip the lever. The calculated final push angle is approximately 76.34° in either direction. The active arm first meets the starting lever position at approximately 67.69° and follows it across the nominal travel. At the final contact, the surface point is 8.99 mm above the modeled pivot, inside the clipped bar's 6–14 mm height, and the closest lever-axis point is 8.47 mm from that pivot.

Measure the purchased switch's actual trip position and force, calibrate a low-speed push and return the servo to centre. The displayed angle is a geometric inspection position, not a programmed endpoint or force limit. The contact-height, opening and sweep settings belong to **Guided fork** and do not adjust this fixed rotor geometry.

Fit the two TPU frames into their seats and install the M3 nuts in the lower cradle. Locate the servo and switch in that cradle, engage its collars with the upper flange and fit the lid with the four through-bolts before fitting the rotor. Assemble the rotor and four disc fasteners off the servo so the small nuts remain accessible, then refit the disc with the supplied shaft retaining hardware through the rotor's central opening. Remove the rotor before lifting the lid for servicing.

## Parallel-shaft rocker · two switches

Select **Parallel-shaft rocker · two switches** for **ARM 1**, the manual guarded switch on the left, and **ARM 2**, the servo-operated switch on the right. View the unit from its marked edge: X points right and Y points away from the operator. ## Guided fork

The four guided examples preserve the larger mounting and linkage design. The default guided dimensions are a 160 × 96 mm base and a switch case mounting face 50 mm above the base bottom. Switching to this mechanism initializes its 10 mm contact height, 13 mm opening and 50° servo push angle.

### Mounting and components

- The common base is 6 mm Al6061-T6 plate. Four external slots are 4.5 mm wide and 12 mm long, aligned with X. Their enclosure-mounting bolts, washers and nuts are selected for the actual wall thickness and are not installed in the model. Use a backing plate where the enclosure wall would flex. Eight separate Ø4.3 mm passages retain the servo cage and deck supports; each has a bottom Ø8.2 × 4.2 mm counterbore, leaving a 1.8 mm screw-head seat.
- A fitted Al6061-T6 cage uses a rear plate, front bridge, integral contact pads and four hard-stop columns to retain the servo with M4 through-bolts. Match the column stack and fitted shims to the delivered case so tightening controls its compression. The cage uses bottom-inserted M4 screws, top Ø9 × 0.8 mm washers and thin AF7 × 2.2 mm M4 nuts (DIN 439 / ISO 4035). The default cage uses 65 mm screws; the raised 65 mm switch platform uses 80 mm screws. Fit or trim a longer stock screw for intermediate heights so the tip clears the moving fork. ST3215 has no projecting RC-servo ears. Its Ø1.6 face pilots carry no mounting screws in this design. Keep cable access and verify free servo motion after tightening opposite tie bolts progressively.
- The switch has an M12 × 0.75 bushing, a keyed Ø12.2 mm panel opening and a 3 mm mounting plate. In the switch's local coordinates the case face is Z=0, the lower nut ends at Z=3, the plate occupies Z=3–6 and the upper nut occupies Z=6–8.4. Platform height moves this complete mounting stack.
- Two hardened Ø6 h6 ground steel rods, each 72 mm long, guide the POM-C fork along X. Rod axes are at Y=±22 mm. The fork has Ø6.2 mm sliding bores; its 12 mm stock is stepped to a 4 mm drive spine. Fixed aluminium guide blocks have Ø6.1 mm rod passages, top M3 × 0.5 clamp screws and bottom M4 × 0.7 deck-retaining threads. The modeled thread bores are nominal major-diameter envelopes; choose tap-drill sizes and machining depths from the component thread callouts. Specify and verify the actual sliding fit.
- An Al6061-T6 Ø24 × 2 mm adapter fits the supplied output disc using four M1.6 × 0.35 through-fasteners on a Ø14 mm pitch circle. Its central Ø8 opening clears the servo's retaining screw. The three ordinary adapter bolts are M1.6×6; the longer M1.6×16 crank bolt retains a turned brass CW614N sleeve at R7. The sleeve has a Ø4 outside, Ø2.2 inside, 9.5 mm stem and Ø6 × 0.5 mm flange. It runs in the fork's 4.3 mm transverse slot to convert crank movement into fork travel. All four AF3.2 × 1.3 mm nuts have a flat facing the central hub, leaving a nominal 0.10 mm hub clearance. Verify the delivered disc, nut orientation, screw engagement and running clearances over the complete sweep.
- Replaceable unfilled TPU 95A pads contact the lever over a 4 mm height. Each pad has a rear flange retained by two M2×8 bolts and two captive hexagon nuts. The default clear opening is 13 mm. Keep access to operate the toggle by hand. This moving fork uses the bare switch; the SAC-01 protective cover belongs to a different installation.

### Dimensions and adjustment

The default base is 160 × 96 mm. The switch case mounting face is 50 mm above the base bottom, so its 3 mm mounting plate occupies Z=53–56 mm. Contact height is 10 mm above the modeled toggle pivot and the servo push angle is 50° from centre. An R7 crank gives approximately 5.36 mm travel from centre at that angle.

The servo output axis is at X=−56 mm and the switch bushing axis at X=34 mm, a 90 mm centre distance. The switch's 28 mm case length, actuator travel and sliding fork follow X. The base dimensions change the external mounting footprint while these internal X coordinates remain fixed.

The ASW drawing dimensions its Ø7 lever and 24° total travel. It does not locate the actual internal pivot or state operating force. The assembly uses the switch model's nominal pivot plane, 12.5 mm above its case face, and checks contact over 4 mm of pad height. The default geometry has approximately 0.37 mm clearance at rest, 0.31 mm movement reserve at the contact midplane and 0.74 mm maximum proposed pad compression.

Adjust **Contact height**, **Fork opening** and **Servo push angle** together. Validation requires at least 0.15 mm clearance in both rest positions, at least 0.15 mm movement reserve at the contact midplane and no more than 1 mm pad compression. These geometry checks permit initial fitting; actual detent travel, printed pad stiffness, tolerances and wear still need measurement.

Before powered use, measure the purchased switch's trip positions and force. Calibrate the push endpoints and pad compression, then set low enough speed, acceleration and torque limits to stop the drive before a hard end load. Return to centre after each push and verify that the switch remains in its maintained position. No servo torque figure is used as an assembly load rating.

At rest the TPU pads have flat contact faces. In a press stage, a tilted Ø7.1 mm lever-clearance shape is subtracted from the active pad to represent a deformed clearance envelope. The geometry provides a contact-clearance study; pad force and deformation require physical measurement or a separate material analysis.

For this mechanism, **Separate components** lifts the switch and mounting deck by 20 mm, and the guide blocks, rods, fork, pads, crank parts and associated drive hardware by 60 mm. The base, servo and retaining cage stay in place. The purchased servo's internals stay assembled in every mechanism; inspection offsets leave the installed component placements and procurement identities unchanged.

## Sources and scope

The [standard ST3215 supplier documentation](https://www.waveshare.com/wiki/ST3215_Servo) supplies the native servo CAD and disc interface. This assembly selects the **ST3215 12 V** library unit as one purchased component; ST3215-HS and ST3215-7.4V are different electrical variants. Eight original STEP solids provide the servo internals, covers and supplied disc pair, with the documented rear-cover topology repair. The ninth visible component, index 8, **Output retaining screw (CAD-fit reference)**, is an existing constructed reference with a smooth nominal Ø2.88 × 3.2 mm shaft envelope and an assumed Ø5 × 1 mm head. Its OEM thread diameter and pitch remain unverified. Measure the delivered retaining screw before specifying replacement hardware.

The [Würth DIN 439 M4 catalog](https://eshop.wuerth.de/Hexagon-nut-low-profile-DIN-439-steel-04-plain-NUT-HEX-DIN439-B-04-WS7-M4/03114.sku/en/US/EUR/) gives the guided cage nut envelope of AF7 × 2.2 mm. Select the fastener property class for the measured application loads.

The [ST servo memory table](https://docs.waveshare.com/Memory_Map_Explanation/ST_Servo_Memory_Map_Explanation) documents position, speed, acceleration and torque-limit controls. It does not establish appropriate force limits for this particular switch or TPU pad.

The [DAIER ASW-07D-1 drawing](https://www.chinadaier.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/ASW-07D-1.pdf) defines the selected switch's 28 × 16 × 19 mm case, 12.5 mm bushing projection, lever and terminal interfaces. The switch package records conflicts in other public envelopes and ratings. This assembly assigns no electrical load rating or operating-force value from those inconsistent listings.

The printed mounting and rocker, and the guided fork's fabricated components, are prototype designs. Component manufacturing definitions, fits, material grades, contact behaviour, control calibration and assembly acceptance remain part of the engineering release work.
