# Servo toggle actuator

Find **Electronics → Switches & buttons → Servo toggle actuator**. The default **Printed rocker** mounts a standard ST3215 12 V bus servo and a bare DAIER ASW-07D using three printed parts. **Parallel-shaft rocker** provides a narrower arrangement with a horizontal rotor and the servo shaft parallel to the switch bushing. **Guided fork** retains the larger assembly with guide rods and soft contact pads. All three use the purchased servo and switch library units.

## Operation

Select **OFF → Press ON → ON → Press OFF → OFF** to inspect a complete cycle. At either rest stage the servo is centred and the lever has clearance. A press stage moves a rounded finger or soft pad against one side of the lever. The mechanism returns to centre while the maintained toggle remains on or off.

The stages show static contact positions. Measure the delivered switch's pivot, force and trip positions before calibrating powered motion. The displayed endpoints do not program the servo or establish a force limit.
