# SP waterproof connectors

Find **Connectors & interfaces → Power & industrial → Power & industrial connectors**. The ten fixed configurations are SP16-5 and SP20-2/3/4/6, each as a straight cable plug with male pins or a round panel socket with female contacts. Housing style and contact sex remain separate searchable characteristics. The model selector chooses a manufactured identity; dimensions are not editable.

The evidence is the supplier's own product listings for kits [00000702](https://klmaster.com/ua/catalog/germetichnyy-razem-sp20-2-pin-ip68-komplekt-702/), [00000705](https://klmaster.com/ua/catalog/germetichnyy-razem-sp20-4-pin-ip68-komplekt-705/) and [00000707](https://klmaster.com/ua/catalog/germetichnyy-razem-sp20-6-pin-ip68-komplekt-707/). The added supplier listings are [SP16-5 kit 00000182](https://klmaster.com/catalog/germetichnyy-razem-sp16-5-pin-182/) and [SP20-3 kit 00000703](https://klmaster.com/catalog/germetichnyy-razem-sp20-3-pin-ip68-komplekt-703/). These identify unbranded SP16 and SP20 families. The manufacturer is not established. Each listing sells the complete plug/socket kit; splitting its two halves into useful library configurations does not establish separate supplier SKUs. There is no substitution with the different WEIPU SP17 or SP21 families.

SP16-5 uses its own [housing drawing](https://klmaster.com/files/goods/182/gallery/germetichnyy_razem_sp16_5_pin_1.jpg), rather than scaling the SP20 envelope. Its explicit dimensions are a **60 mm plug**, **22 mm socket body**, **Ø25 collar**, nominal **Ø16 mounting barrel**, and **Ø16.5 panel opening with opposing flats 15.3 mm apart**. The cable-entry drawing is marked Ø8 / PG9. Thread pitch is not established. The [SP16 contact table](https://klmaster.com/files/goods/182/gallery/germetichnyy_razem_sp16_5_pin_ip68_komplekt_2.jpg) gives five Ø1 mm contacts around a ring with no central contact. The authored pitch radius is 3.4 mm; the source does not dimension it. Labels 1 and 2 are upper left/right, 3 and 4 lower left/right, and 5 bottom centre. Exact angular registration to the key remains approximate.

The [SP16 front photograph](https://klmaster.com/files/goods/182/big_germetichnyy_razem_sp16_5_pin_ip68_komplekt.jpg) confirms female mating contacts in the panel socket and male pins in the cable plug. The protruding gold features in the supplier's rear-view image are solder cups, not evidence of a male panel execution. Both series therefore retain the same panel-female / cable-male convention in this library.

The [supplier contact table](https://klmaster.com/files/goods/707/gallery/germetichnyy_razem_sp20_6_pin_ip68_komplekt_1.jpg) establishes two or four Ø2.8 mm contacts and six Ø1.8 mm contacts. Its face diagrams establish the topology: two contacts in a line, four around a circle and six around a circle. Pitch, angular numbering, key dimensions, engagement depth and female sleeve clearance are not dimensioned. The gold sockets, recessed pins and rear solder cups are illustrative. A four-contact photograph also appears in the six-contact listing, so the six-contact configuration follows the table and the listing's six-contact photograph, not that reused image.

For the panel socket, Z=0 is the flange rear seat and +Z points outside the enclosure. The two opposing rear barrel flats face ±Y. The nominal Ø20 SP20 or Ø16 SP16 rear barrel occupies −8..0 mm. Authored barrel clearances place the SP20 flat faces 19.0 mm apart and the SP16 faces 14.8 mm apart, within the sourced panel openings; these barrel-flat values are fitting assumptions, not additional published callouts. The **Host panel thickness** control moves the inner retaining nut so its outer face is at −t. Its 0.8–4 mm range is an installation study; neither this permitted range nor the approximate nut dimensions are verified supplier limits. Authored nuts are 25 mm AF for SP20 and 21 mm AF for SP16, both 3.5 mm thick. The seal sits in a matching recess at Z=0..0.5 mm and does not occupy the host panel volume.

For the plug, Z=0 is the front protective shroud plane and the cable exits toward −Z. The male pin tips are recessed to −3 mm. The 66 mm SP20 and 60 mm SP16 overall envelopes are sourced. Undimensioned coupling and gland diameters, body sections, nut lengths and axial subdivisions are approximate. SP16 has independent mount, collar and length values; the shared shape recipe scales only undimensioned internal radial features. Housing threads, cable, cap, tether, wires and the host panel are omitted. The inspection state separates the gland and coupling nuts or the panel nut and seal; it does not add the mating half or change procurement quantity.

Preview and native FreeCAD recipe share one package-owned shape recipe. Each colored piece is an independent solid component. The preview and export preserve the same placement and envelope in both installed and inspection views. The supplied cap and flexible cable are absent from both, so their clearance must be checked separately.

The supplier advertises IP68 and publishes electrical figures, but other SP sources conflict on voltage, cable range and operating temperature. No electrical rating or ingress qualification is provided as a searchable fact in this package. The geometry establishes no immersion depth, duration, seal compression, thread fit, mating interchangeability or completed enclosure protection. Confirm the actual delivered product before releasing an installation.
