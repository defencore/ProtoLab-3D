# Machine control components

Fixed external dimensions follow each linked manufacturer document. Terminal contours, fascia and vent positions are simplified. Selected mounting interfaces are explicit below; housing contours remain illustrations. Revision CL57T V3.0 must not be confused with later versions.

The complete configuration includes `panelThickness` (1 to 6 mm, default 4 mm). This is the installed XB5 clamp setting, not a source-certified fixed dimension of the purchased product. Use the complete defaults or a preset when generating any selected model.

The [ATV12HU22M2 drawing](https://iportal.se.com/Contents/docs/SQD-ATV12HU22M2.PDF), page 5, publishes two diagonal diameter-5 fixing holes on a 93 by 120 mm pattern. In native coordinates they are X/Z −46.5/137 and +46.5/17 mm. The 3 mm rear tab thickness and open screw-head contour are illustrative and require delivered drawing approval.

The [LRS-150 mechanical drawing](https://www.meanwell.com/Upload/PDF/LRS-150/LRS-150-SPEC.PDF), Case 241A, publishes bottom two M3 holes, depth 3 mm, at X 24 and 102 mm from the left and 32 mm from the bottom. Native coordinates are X −55.5/+22.5, Z 32, rear Y 0. The CNC panel uses M3 by 6 screws through its 4 mm thickness: 2 mm engagement and 1 mm unused depth. Confirm installed orientation and cooling derating with the supplier.

The [PNOZ X3 installation manual](https://www.pilz.com/download/open/PNOZ_X3_Operating_Manual_20547-EN-10.pdf), page 10, requires a 35 mm DIN rail, retention for a vertical rail, and an IP54 cabinet. The nominal rear rail recess/capture represents that mounting method; exact latch contours are unpublished and need checking on the supplied relay. The CNC adds a horizontal rail and two side stops, with accessible fixing screws outside the relay body. The rail section is an editable stock-section illustration; gauge the delivered section and check positive latch retention before use.

The [XB4/XB5 mounting sheet](https://www.se.com/uk/en/download/document/BRU46063/) gives the panel hole and installation sequence. The XB5AS8444 complete product includes its plastic fixing collar; it is not a separate fabricated M22 nut. A recommended 22.5 mm hole and 1 to 6 mm panel are represented. The modeled collar rear face and operator head front face clamp the selected panel thickness with positive face contact; detailed collar/locking-screw contours remain illustrative. Use the delivered instructions, specified tightening torque and anti-rotation provisions. Do not infer a fabricated thread pitch from the barrel diameter.

The CNC DDCS installation uses a panel aperture 1 mm larger than the modeled rear enclosure and four authored rear clamp straps and spacers. This provides positive fascia/panel and rear enclosure/strap contact without inventing OEM screw holes. The aperture is a design choice pending measurement, rather than a verified manufacturer's drill template. The source-only Huanyang 4 kW execution has no verified mounting-hole coordinates: its CNC fixture uses a captive external cradle and removable upper stop, preserving the purchased housing. Approve the delivered housing, airflow and connector access before manufacturing that fixture.

CNC captured nuts use an explicit Full-only 0.05 mm radial CAD inspection allowance, while retaining nominal metric diameter, pitch and span. Simple keeps the nominal bore; catalogue nuts keep zero by default. The allowance separates the Full inspection envelopes from ideal coincident flanks; it is not a supplier-certified ISO fit class, a tap-drill or manufacturing instruction. Inspect the delivered nut/screw fit and approve the actual fastening/preload specification. Native checks retain the same strict intersection threshold and include deliberate phase errors that must report real overlap.

A PLC handles normal sequencing. The safety relay, guard locking, standstill monitoring, contactor feedback and pneumatic load holding require a separate validated design. The ATV12 does not supply an assumed safety-rated STO function. Keep guards locked during blade coast-down and keep the work clamped until the blade is safely retracted and stopped when required by the risk assessment.

The ATV12HU22M2 input line current exceeds 20 A at full rating. A 230 V supply description does not establish that a 16 A branch circuit is adequate.

DDCS v4.1 is available as a separate model with its fascia, screen and 17 keys, alongside the two-NC Schneider XB5AS8444 emergency stop. DDCS supplies the motion/HMI role; its three 50 mA sinking outputs cannot directly drive the saw's pneumatic sequence. Use a process PLC with sufficient I/O and electrically compatible isolated handshakes. Its manual's panel-cutout text conflicts with the adjacent drawing, so verify the delivered unit before machining the console.
