# Rocket bulkhead

Single circular plate. Set the central clearance-hole diameter to zero for a solid disk; a positive value makes a concentric through hole. Z is the thickness axis; the origin is at the plate midpoint. The hole is a generic clearance feature. No threads, seals, pressure rating, recovery anchor or hardware are implied by this geometry.

## LDRS use

A reusable passive detail for high power hobby rocketry layouts and preparation for Large Dangerous Rocket Ships (LDRS). The package supplies a single physical component with editable millimetre dimensions.

## Presets and evidence

The three metric presets are author-defined prototype examples. They are not manufacturer parts, measured products or published standard sizes, and intentionally carry no catalog evidence. Select actual tube and stock dimensions for the project instead of inferring commercial compatibility from a preset name.

## Geometry and export

Preview, binary STL and FreeCAD export describe the same nominal detail. The FreeCAD recipe creates one solid. Reported dimensions are the outside diameter on X/Y and the axial length or plate thickness on Z. The model has only the default physical state.
