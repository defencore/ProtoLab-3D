# Li-Po / LiHV battery packs

16 fixed manufacturer models. Units: mm, V, mAh, A.

Published pack dimensions; cables, plugs and balance leads excluded. Wrapper seams, end folds and label regions are illustrative, not dimensioned in the source. Connector type is not specified by this series table. Published C-rate is not a verified harness current rating.

Model selection controls all dimensions; filters never resize a physical product. All source URLs and rating conditions are stored per preset. Unknown capacity/current is omitted, never represented as zero. Geometry and FreeCAD export use the same component definitions.

The additional `lipo-battery:tattu-3s-650-75c` is the FPV Classic XT30 execution used by Rocket Release: nominal 58 × 31 × 16 mm, 59 g, 11.1 V and 650 mAh. Its manufacturer product page publishes length ±5 mm, width/height ±2 mm and mass ±20 g; these are retained as source conditions, not simulated tolerances. Supplied 45 mm leads, XT30 and JST-XHR-4P plugs remain part of one purchased pack but their undimensioned geometry is omitted. The existing 2S ID and 58 mm Grepow series-table length are preserved; the exact XT30 product page says 57 mm. The Rocket Release 57 mm fit-reference layout is not silently resized or certified by this standalone registration.
